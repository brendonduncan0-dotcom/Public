> [!info] Read Aloud
> Steep wooden stairs drop into a low-ceilinged cellar that smells of tallow, damp earth and fear-sweat. It's utterly dark: not a candle burns, in a candle-maker's house. Somewhere ahead, someone is breathing very fast and trying very hard not to.

## Notable Features

- **Ball bearings:** scattered across the bottom three stairs.
- **A bell string:** tied across the stairway at ankle height to a cowbell on a beam.
- **A bucket on the inner door:** a wooden bucket balanced on top of the half-open door between the two rooms.
- **Racks of unlit candles:** hundreds of tallow candles hanging by their wicks from ceiling racks, none lit.
- **Bedrolls and a chamber pot:** three straw bedrolls laid close together in the back room; the family has been living down here by day for months.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Perception:** the bucket on the door is full of flour.
- **DC 10 Investigation (back room):** a stack of soot-blackened rags and a pot of cellar mud; the family has been treating sunburn with a home remedy.
- **DC 13 Investigation (shelf):** a tin of fine lamp-black, with a scrawled label: *"For Mara — for the blades."* (A clue that leads to [[Mara Thatch]].)

> [!tip] DM Note
> The Rush family will talk freely once convinced the party is human. They confirm the town wakes at dusk, recommend Mara and Dorrie, and warn the party never to break another door by day. If the party shelters here until dusk, the family offers them cold tea and a stale bun, and the levity of being hosted by people who tried to brain them five minutes ago should play well.
