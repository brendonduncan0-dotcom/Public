> [!info] Read Aloud
> The contracts officer drops a sack on the desk. It clinks, but not like gold: like coppers, silvers, a few old rings, a child's tin whistle. "From Gildenfield," she says. "The whole town chipped in. The temple of Lathander matched it, after they read your report." She unfolds a letter written in three different hands. The last line is underlined so hard the quill tore the paper: *Please come back. Please finish it.*

## Setup
This short scene delivers the guild contract and recaps [[Oneshot 4 - Sunblind]]. If this group didn't play Oneshot 4, the contracts officer reads them the briefing below.

> **Guild Contract — Faerûn Adventure League**
> **Clients:** The townsfolk of Gildenfield (Mara Thatch, Dorrie Nightjar, Osric Loam, signing), with a matching bounty from the Temple of Lathander, Waterdeep
> **Objective:** End the threat of the "glarewights" in Gildenfield and restore the town to daylight.
> **Reward:** 500 gp on completion. **Bonus:** 100 gp from the Temple of Lathander if Father Aldric is returned alive to face the temple's judgement.

**Recap / briefing (read or paraphrase):** Gildenfield's priest, Father Aldric, trapped a sliver of a deva named Ilvaris in a mirrored reliquary years ago. This summer the reliquary cracked. The escaped light became the glarewights, radiant creatures that hide in bright light and are immune to ordinary weapons in the sun, but vulnerable in darkness. The rest of the light went into Aldric, who now serves it by day and begs for help at dusk. The town lives by night. Soot-blackened weapons, flour, and darkness are the weapons that work.

## Objectives
- Accept the contract.
- Gather any last supplies from the guild's shops before setting out (consumables only).

## NPC Goals
The contracts officer wants this job taken seriously; it's the first contract a whole town has ever pooled for.
