**Act:** 2 — [[Act 2 - The Town Wakes]]
**Location:** [[The Late Supper]]
**Encounter type:** Social

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The Late Supper is warm, low-beamed and lit by candles in shuttered tin lanterns, every flame turned down to a glow. The place is packed: farmers in their nightclothes eating breakfast, a card game, someone playing a fiddle very quietly. A chalkboard over the bar reads "SUNRISE IN: 9 HOURS. DRINK ACCORDINGLY." The vegetables in the stew are enormous, glossy and perfect. The woman behind the bar points a ladle at you. "Sit. Eat. Then you can tell me what you want."

## Setup
This is the investigation hub of Act 2. Let the party mingle and gather clues for 20–30 minutes of real time, then point them toward the night's three big leads: Mara, the Fennimores, and the temple. Present at the tavern:

- **[[Dorrie Nightjar]]** behind the bar: rumours, gallows humour, safe rooms.
- **[[Pip]]**, Dorrie's niece, carrying bowls; will talk if approached kindly (especially with [[Captain]]).
- **[[Osric Loam]]** alone at a corner table with his black shovel propped beside him: the burial rite, the ledger.
- **Ned Dunmore**, the miller (minor NPC): a floury, mournful man who confirms the grain is in his mill and paid for, but says nobody moves a cart in daylight, and he can't load forty sacks alone in the dark. "Tomorrow at dusk. If you're still alive. No offence."

## Objectives
- Learn what is happening in Gildenfield (as much as the party can piece together).
- Learn the burial rite in time to bury Wendel before dawn.
- Arrange to collect the grain (Ned will load it at dusk tomorrow).
- Find a lead toward the temple and Aldric's journal.

## NPC Goals
- **Dorrie:** keep everyone fed and calm; quietly hope the party can actually help.
- **Pip:** be believed.
- **Osric:** keep the dead in the ground. If the party has a body with them (Wendel), he wants it buried *properly* and *tonight*.
- **Ned:** get Hobb's grain off his hands before something happens to it, or to him.

## Possible Outcomes
- **The party has their leads:** Point them to Mara ([[Mara's Lesson]]), the Fennimores ([[The Glowing Son]]) and the temple ([[The Vestry Heist]]). They can take these in any order through the night. Keep an eye on the clock: [[Before Dawn]] must happen before sunrise.
- **The party rests here:** Dorrie gives them a cellar room. A short rest is easy; a long rest would eat the whole night and the burial. Make that trade-off clear.
- **Levity beats:** Rindle tasting the "sunburn tea" (the remedy, which a farmer is drinking out of desperation: "It's not for drinking." "*It's not for drinking?*"); the card game, where every player is cheating and everyone knows; Dorrie charging Captain for a saucer of cream.
