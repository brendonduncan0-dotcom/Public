**Act:** 3 — [[Act 3 - The Noon Service]]
**Location:** [[The Temple Garden]]
**Encounter type:** Combat (with trap hazards)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The temple bell has stopped. In the silence the temple garden blazes under the noon sun: gravel paths white as salt, flowers turned toward the dome, and everywhere, on posts and arches and the rims of still stone basins, mirrors. Hundreds of them, angled and gleaming, throwing light into every corner until there are no shadows at all. The great doors of the temple stand open at the top of the path. From inside comes the sound of a man's voice, warm and gentle, leading a hymn. Nobody is singing back. And Captain, who would not come within thirty feet of this place two nights ago, walks past you up the path.

## Setup
The garden is Aldric's own defence: packed with his mirror-lantern rigs, because he believes light protects him. At noon the whole garden is **bright light**. Two glarewights drift among the mirrors, late for service.

**Captain walks in.** Make this a moment. The cat that refused to approach the temple now leads the way, tail high. If Rook asks, Captain doesn't answer. It just keeps walking.

**The mirror rigs.** Three rig clusters stand along the path (south, centre, north). Each has a tripwire:
- **Spot:** DC 13 Perception (Advantage if the party scouted the temple in [[Preparing for Noon]]).
- **Disarm:** DC 12 thieves' tools, or smash the mirrors (AC 11, 5 HP each cluster).
- **Triggered:** the rig flares and sweeps a blinding beam across the path; each creature within 15 feet makes a DC 12 Constitution save or is **Blinded** until the end of its next turn, and the glarewights immediately converge on the flare.

**Shade.** There is almost none, by design. The party can make their own: pitch smoke pots, Darkness, Fog Cloud, or a soot cloth stretched between two of them (an action; creates a 5-foot square of dim light while held). The temple doors' deep porch at the top of the path is the only natural shade.

## Objectives
- Reach the temple doors.
- Avoid or disarm the mirror rigs.
- Deal with the two glarewights without spending everything you've got before the boss.

## Enemies
| Creature | CR | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Glarewight]] | 1 | 2 | Converge on any flared rig; ignore anyone in smoke or shade |

**Terrain features:** See [[The Temple Garden]]. Mirror basins, rig posts and arches; the porch at the north end is dim.

> [!warning] Scaling
> - **Resource check:** this fight should cost a little, not a lot. If the party is spending big spells here, have the glarewights break off and drift inside to "service" after 3 rounds.
> - **Stronger:** If the party skipped preparation, add a third glarewight.
> - **Ally:** If Town Trust is 2+, [[Mara Thatch]] comes with them this far, throwing flour pouches and taking crossbow shots. At the doors she stops: "That's your job. I'll hold the door."

## Possible Outcomes
- **Clean approach:** The party reaches the porch with resources intact. They can see into the sanctum from the doorway; read the opening of [[The Noon Service]] and let them plan for one round before Aldric notices them.
- **Rigs flared:** The party arrives blinded, burned and noticed. Aldric's hymn stops. "Ah. You've come to service." Initiative starts immediately.
