**Act:** 1 — [[Act 1 - The Silent Town]]
**Location:** [[Cooper's Row]]
**Encounter type:** Combat

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Cooper's Row is a narrow, sun-baked lane of workshops and shuttered houses. Halfway down, a grain wagon stands abandoned in the middle of the street, its traces empty and its horse long gone. The wall behind it has been bleached bone-white, as if a hundred summers struck it at once, except for one patch in the exact shape of a man with his arms thrown up, still the old brick-red. There's no smell of smoke. Only hot stone, and something like linen drying in the sun.

## Setup
It is early afternoon. The street is in full, bright sunlight except for the shade under a row of shop awnings on the east side (dim light) and the open, pitch-dark doorway of the cooperage on the west side (darkness inside). Wendel Pike's wagon stands in the middle of the street; his body lies beneath it, drained pale and peeled as if by years of sun.

**Phase 1: the trapped street (exploration).** Before the ambush, give the party a few minutes to investigate. This is the session's trap/hazard challenge; the town's traps should first be met as hazards, then become tools.

| Trap | Where | Spot | Effect | Turn it into a weapon |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ball bearings | Cooperage doorway and the bakery doorway | Visible (no check) | DC 10 Dex save or fall Prone when moving through | Kick a handful into a wight's path (action): DC 10 Dex save or it's knocked Prone. Wights hover low and still get tripped. |
| Bear trap under straw | Beside the wagon's south wheel | DC 13 Perception | 2d4 Piercing and Restrained; DC 13 Strength (Athletics) to escape | Lure a wight over it. |
| Bell strings & tin cans | Across three front doors | Visible | Harmless; the household inside bangs pots and screams "WE'RE ARMED!" (levity) | A noise distraction. |
| Mirror-lantern rig | On a post at the north end, tripwire across the street | DC 13 Perception to spot, DC 12 thieves' tools to disarm | Lanterns flare behind angled mirrors, throwing bright light under the east awnings: the shade becomes bright light | Smash the mirrors (AC 11, 3 HP each) to deny the wights. |
| Flour sacks | In the wagon bed (6 sacks) | Visible | — | A sack thrown (action, 20 ft.) reveals every wight within 5 ft. of where it lands. |

**Phase 2: the ambush.** Three glarewights drift in on the light while the party is examining the wagon or the body. Give a fair warning first: dust motes on the wagon bed flare like sparks; heat haze shimmers in a sunlit corner where nothing is hot; [[Captain]] puffs up and hisses at empty air. Then roll initiative.

## Objectives
- Survive the ambush and drive off or destroy the glarewights.
- Recover Wendel's body (it must be buried properly before dawn; see [[Before Dawn]]).
- Work out how the glarewights work: **this is the lesson of the session**.

## Enemies
| Creature | CR | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Glarewight]] | 1 | 3 | Strike from the sun, vanish, repeat; ignore anyone in darkness |

**Terrain features:** See [[Cooper's Row]]. Key light zones: open street (bright), east awnings (dim, unless the mirror rig fires), the cooperage interior (darkness).

> [!tip] DM Note: Teaching the Rule Without Saying It
> Describe every hit by light level, and let the players draw the conclusion.
> - **Bright light:** *"Your blade passes through the shimmer like it's swinging through heat over a forge. Nothing."*
> - **Dim light (awning shade):** *"Under the awning it flickers into view, a man-shaped ripple, and your blade catches and drags, like cutting wet canvas."* (Half damage.)
> - **Darkness (cooperage doorway):** *"In the dark of the doorway it's suddenly there: a pale, glowing figure, all hollow eyes and light. Your axe bites deep."* (Full damage.)
> - **Flour:** *"The flour doesn't fall through it. It clings. For the first time you can see the shape: tall, thin, almost angelic, eyeless."*
> - **Spells always work** (Eldritch Blast, Magic Missile, etc.) if they can be aimed. **Radiant spells heal them**: *"The light pours into it and it brightens, almost gratefully."*
>
> Ways the party can move a wight into the dark: Grapple and drag; Shove it 5 feet; stand in the doorway and attack (wights fly at attackers); or lure it with a lantern into the cooperage, then smash the lantern.

> [!warning] Scaling
> - **If the party is struggling (no one has worked it out by round 3):** Have a wight chase someone into awning shade, so the party sees it flicker; have a frightened voice shout from behind a barred door, "Get them in the dark! The *dark*!" (It's old Agnes Rush in [[The Chandler's Cellar]]; she won't open the door.)
> - **If it's too easy:** Add a fourth glarewight, or have a wight trigger the mirror-lantern rig, flooding the awning shade with bright light.
> - Glarewights flee at 5 HP or less. If two have fled or been destroyed, the rest withdraw.

## Possible Outcomes
- **The party learns to use darkness:** The wights are destroyed or flee. Destroyed glarewights don't leave bodies: they flare, bleach the cobbles in a starburst, and are gone. The party now knows the core rule. Celebrate it.
- **The party retreats indoors:** The wights won't follow into darkness. The fight ends; the party has learned the rule the other way round.
- **Sun sickness:** Anyone who failed a Searing Touch save has lost Constitution. Describe the symptoms (peeling skin, thirst, no sweat) to make it feel real; it recovers on a long rest or with Mara's remedy in Act 2.
- **Wendel:** The party finds his body and his last delivery note (*"Mill shut. Town shut. Priest says wait for service. Will wait in cooperage shade till dusk."*). He didn't make it to the shade.
