**Role:** Patron (Rook's Celestial patron, disguised as his pet cat)
**First appears:** Act 1
**Location:** Travels with Rook

## Personality
- Aloof, smug and fastidious, in the way of all cats; this is a convenient disguise for something very old
- Protective of Rook in a way that looks like indifference until it suddenly isn't
- In Gildenfield: uneasy, then grieving; it recognises the light here

## Appearance
A sleek, well-fed tabby that Rook sees as an ordinary cat; in strong light or at certain angles, other people occasionally glimpse the folded outline of wings.

## Motivation
Captain is Rook's celestial patron wearing the shape of a cat. The deva-light trapped in Gildenfield belongs to **Ilvaris**, a deva Captain once knew: a companion, a sibling-flame, or perhaps a piece of Captain's own light lent long ago (leave the exact relationship for the finale in [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]]). Captain wants Ilvaris's light recovered and doesn't fully trust mortals to do it gently. It may want the light *returned to it* rather than freed, a quiet tension only Rook's player needs to feel.

## Secret
Captain can see glarewights perfectly well in bright light, and knows exactly what Father Aldric did. It will never say so out loud, at least not until the very end of [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]].

## Reactions to Use

Captain is the adventure's fairest warning system. Use it consistently so the players learn to trust the cat.

- **Arrival:** ears flat, tail low, won't walk on sunlit cobbles if there's shade available.
- **Staring at nothing:** tracks empty air, puffs up at sunny corners, hisses at empty doorways. There is always a glarewight there.
- **Around Aldric:** purrs loudly, almost warningly, and won't take its eyes off him.
- **The temple:** refuses to go within 30 feet of the temple doors; plants its feet and yowls if carried.
- **[[Tam Fennimore]]:** gentle and unafraid; curls up on his chest. A hint that he can be saved.
- **Radiant spells:** the first time Rook casts a radiant spell at a glarewight and heals it, let it happen. The second time, Captain swats his casting hand or gives him a long, pointed stare.
- **Twilight:** at dusk, Captain sits facing the temple, very still.

## In Combat

- **Once per combat:** Captain can bat at a hidden glarewight within 30 feet, revealing it (it is no longer hidden), or yowl to give Rook Advantage on one Wisdom (Perception) or Search check.
- Captain can't be targeted in this adventure; it is always somewhere sensible (on a beam, under a cart, in Rook's pack).

## Warmth and Grief

Whenever Rook is near the temple, the reliquary, or a glarewight that is dying in the dark, describe a private feeling to his player: warmth, familiarity, a grief that isn't his. It is Captain's grief. Don't explain it.
