**Act:** 3 — [[Act 3 - The Noon Service]]
**Location:** [[The Sun Temple]]
**Encounter type:** Social (resolution)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> On the altar, among the shattered mirrors, the cracked crystal lies pulsing with a faint, steady light, warm as a heartbeat. Through the torn dome, ordinary noon sunlight falls onto the floor: just sunlight, harmless, golden, dust turning slowly in it. Captain walks to the altar, sits beside the crystal, and looks at you. Waiting.

## Setup
The fight is over. What is left of Ilvaris's stolen light is in the crystal. The party must decide what to do with it. Give them time to talk it over; this is the choice the whole two-parter has been building toward. Rook's player may feel a pull none of the others do.

## Objectives
- Decide the fate of the deva's light.
- Decide (or discover) Aldric's fate.

## The Three Choices

### Free It
Carry the crystal into the open sunlight beneath the torn dome and shatter it.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The crystal breaks with a sound like a bell, very far away. The light rises from it, and rises, and gathers, and becomes a tall, thin figure in soft gold, translucent and tired, with folded wings and a face that is exactly as kind as the townsfolk remember. It looks at its own hands. Then it looks at you, and smiles.

See [[Ilvaris]] for everything the deva says and does. In short: it blesses the party (Heroic Inspiration and healing of all sun sickness), heals everyone touched in Gildenfield including [[Tam Fennimore]], forgives Aldric (and wakes him if he is dying), bows to Captain, and rises through the dome and is gone. **This is the full-victory ending.**

### Give It to the Patron
Rook (or anyone) offers the crystal to Captain.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Captain stands. For a moment, just a moment, everyone in the room sees the wings: great folded wings of pale gold, filling half the sanctum. Then the cat looks up at Rook, and speaks, for the first and only time, in a voice like a sunrise.

Choose the tone that suits your table:
- **Sweet:** *"Thank you, Rook. I lent this light long ago, to a friend who went to help some frightened people. I've missed it. I'll keep it safe until Ilvaris is strong enough to take it back."* Captain touches its nose to the crystal; the light flows into the cat. Every glarewight in the valley fades. Rook gains his patron's favour (a boon at your discretion, ideally narrative: a warning dream, a door opened, a debt remembered).
- **Ominous:** *"Good. It was always mine."* The light flows into the cat, and its eyes stay gold a moment too long. Captain says nothing more and is purring by nightfall. Rook may wonder for a long time what his patron really is.

In either case, Aldric is not forgiven by anyone but the town, and Ilvaris remains diminished somewhere: a seed for a future story.

### Keep It
The party takes the crystal.

The light stays caged. The glarewights fade anyway: without Aldric or the Relic to feed them, they gutter out over the next few days. The crystal is warm and hums faintly; Captain refuses to be near it. Per the campaign's rules, it becomes **auction stock for the guild**, not party loot: the guild pays the party a **150 gp finder's fee** and lists it as *"The Gildenfield Shard: floods an area with daylight or swallows it in darkness, once per day."* It is still a captive. **Sequel hook:** whoever buys it at auction, and the "kind stranger" who taught Aldric the mirror geometry, might be very interested.

## Aldric's Fate

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Reached at dawn and talked down, Ilvaris freed | Ilvaris forgives him. He survives, burned, humbled and human. He will go to Waterdeep to face the temple of Lathander's judgement, walking, at dawn. **+100 gp temple bonus.** |
| Talked down, Ilvaris not freed | He survives, but never forgives himself. He goes to Waterdeep anyway. **+100 gp temple bonus.** |
| Reduced to 0 HP, Ilvaris freed | Ilvaris wakes him, and he survives as above. **+100 gp bonus.** |
| Reduced to 0 HP, Ilvaris not freed | He dies before dusk. Osric buries him properly, before dawn, by the rite. The whole town comes. A tragedy. |

## The Town Wakes

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The next day, at noon, there's a knock at your door at The Late Supper. Not three soft taps: a hammering, and voices, and laughter. When you open it, the sun is blazing and the street is full of people. Shutters are flung open up and down the lane. Dorrie is standing on a table in the middle of the street with the chalkboard: *"SUNSET IN: 8 HOURS. WHO CARES."* Mara is pulling up her bear traps and handing them out as souvenirs. Tam Fennimore walks into the sunlight with his father's arm round his shoulders and turns his face up to it with his eyes closed. And all along the verges, every flower in Gildenfield has turned to face the sun.

Adjust to fit what happened (if Tam died, Garrick stands alone in the sun, and lifts a hand to the party).

## Rewards
- **Contract:** 500 gp. **+100 gp** temple bonus if Aldric lives.
- **Mara's keepsake:** one of her bear traps, polished up, with *"FOR LUCK — M."* scratched on the jaw (mundane, but it's a working bear trap).
- **Osric's shovel:** the grave-chill fades now that the glarewights are gone, but he lets the party keep it. "Good shovel. Dig well."
- **Constitution:** all lost Constitution is restored (by Ilvaris's blessing, or by the town's remedy and a long rest).
- **By choice:** Ilvaris's blessing, Captain's favour, or the 150 gp finder's fee for the shard.
- **Bonus:** Town Trust 3+ — Gildenfield sends the guild a cart of its too-perfect produce every season, addressed to the party by name. Hobb Barleycorn insists on carting it for free.
