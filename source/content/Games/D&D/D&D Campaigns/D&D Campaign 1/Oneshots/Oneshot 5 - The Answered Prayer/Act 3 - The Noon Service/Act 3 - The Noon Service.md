At noon, the party walks into the light. They fight through Aldric's mirror-trapped garden, and Captain, who would not come near the temple before, leads the way. In the blazing sanctum, they must make a holy place dark: cut banners, smash lenses, smother lanterns, and cover the reliquary, stripping Aldric's power until they can defeat him or reach the man inside the light. When he falls, the light he was holding pours out into something far worse, and the party must keep the darkness going while protecting Captain from a hunger that wants its light. At the end, a cracked crystal pulses on the altar, and the party decides what becomes of Ilvaris.
