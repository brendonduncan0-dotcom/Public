The party takes Hobb Barleycorn's contract and arrives in Gildenfield at midday to find a town that looks like a summer postcard and feels like a held breath: every door barred, every window blacked out, and a kindly priest lighting lanterns in the sun. Their immediate goal is to find Hobb's carter and his grain wagon. Instead they find Wendel's body beneath a bleached wall, and things their weapons pass straight through. This act teaches the core rule of the adventure (light protects the enemy; darkness exposes it) and sets up the town waking at dusk in Act 2.
