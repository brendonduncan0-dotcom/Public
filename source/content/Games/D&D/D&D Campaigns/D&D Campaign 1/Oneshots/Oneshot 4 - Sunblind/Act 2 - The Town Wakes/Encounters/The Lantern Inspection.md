> [!info] Read Aloud
> As the sun touches the hills, Gildenfield exhales. Bolts slide back up and down the street. Doors creak open a hand's width, then wider. People step out blinking into the dusk: pale, thin, exhausted, carrying shuttered lanterns. Within a minute there's a small crowd in front of the tavern, and it has noticed you. A woman with a soot-streaked face steps forward, opens her lantern's shutter, and holds it up to your face, very close. "Hold still," she says. "Let's see if you're real."

## Setup
At dusk the whole town wakes and gathers outside [[The Late Supper]]. Strangers are checked one by one with a lantern: do they cast a shadow? Do their eyes look normal? Do they flinch from the light? The crowd is led by [[Mara Thatch]], with [[Dorrie Nightjar]] on the tavern steps and [[Osric Loam]] leaning on his black shovel at the back.

Nobody here is hostile, just exhausted and wary. This is the session's main social encounter, and it can be fully resolved without combat.

## Objectives
- Pass the inspection (prove you're human).
- Start earning the town's trust.
- Realise who you chatted with at noon (the moment the townsfolk hear the party *talked* to Aldric).

## NPC Goals
- **Mara:** make sure the strangers aren't sun-ghosts, and find out if they're useful or just more bodies for Osric.
- **Dorrie:** feed them, find out what they want, and get them inside before anyone starts a fight.
- **The crowd:** reassurance. Someone, anyone, who isn't scared.

## Town Trust

Track **Town Trust** for the rest of the adventure (and into [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]]). It starts at **0**.

| Change | Cause |
|---|---|
| +1 | Passing the inspection calmly (no drawn weapons, no complaints) |
| +1 | Bringing Wendel's body in respectfully |
| +1 | Small help: resetting a trap, carrying water, buying a round, fixing a door |
| +1 | Treating [[Tam Fennimore]] |
| −1 | Each door forced by day |
| −2 | Killing a townsperson |

| Trust | Effect |
|---|---|
| **2 or more** | Mara teaches the soot-blades and remedy freely. The Fennimores open their door. Osric shares his ledger. Dorrie's food and rooms are free. |
| **0 to 1** | Normal. Each NPC needs to be won over individually (see their encounters). |
| **−1 or less** | Doors stay shut at dusk, prices double, Mara is cold (DC 15 Persuasion to get anything from her), the Fennimores won't answer. |

## Skill Challenge

| Check | DC | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| (No check) Stand still under the lantern | — | Shadow cast, eyes normal: "Real." +1 Trust if done with grace. | — |
| Persuasion or Performance (Cordelia's moment) | 12 | The crowd relaxes; someone laughs. Mara grunts approval. | A little stiff, but no harm done. |
| Insight | 11 | The crowd isn't hostile; they're *terrified*, and they've been doing this for months. | |
| Any PC with sun sickness | — | The crowd sees peeling skin and goes very quiet. Mara: "You've been touched. How many times?" She notes it, and it earns sympathy, not suspicion. | |

## Possible Outcomes
- **The party mentions talking to Aldric at noon:** The crowd goes silent. Dorrie, very gently: "You *talked* to him? In the day?" Someone crosses themselves. Mara: "And you're still walking. Huh." This is the moment the party realises who they met. Let it land.
- **Passed and welcomed:** Dorrie ushers them into The Late Supper. Move to [[Supper at Sundown]].
- **The party draws weapons or refuses inspection:** The crowd scatters back inside; doors slam. Trust −1. Dorrie still lets them into the tavern, grumbling ("I don't turn away paying custom, even rude custom").
- **Levity beat (Cordelia):** A noble who has never been inspected by peasants with a lantern. Play it for all it's worth. Dorrie: "You're real, love. Real *posh*, but real."
