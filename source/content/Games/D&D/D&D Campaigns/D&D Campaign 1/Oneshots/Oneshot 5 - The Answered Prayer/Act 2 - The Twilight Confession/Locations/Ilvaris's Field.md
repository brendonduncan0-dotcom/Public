> [!info] Read Aloud
> A wide wheat field on the slope below the temple, the stalks taller than a person and heavy with grain, silver with dew in the grey pre-dawn light. In the middle of the field is a perfect circle where nothing grows. Every poppy along the field's edge is turned toward that circle. The air smells of wet earth and, very faintly, of warm bread.

## Notable Features

- **The bare circle:** a ring of earth about thirty feet across in the middle of the wheat, where nothing has grown since the deva first stood here.
- **The wing imprint:** at the circle's centre, bare earth baked pale and glassy in the shape of two great outspread wings.
- **Towering wheat:** dense, golden, taller than a person all around the circle.
- **Turned flowers:** every flower at the field's edge faces the circle, not the sun.
- **The old oak:** a gnarled oak on the eastern edge of the field, with a deep, dark hollow among its roots.
- **The temple on the hill:** visible to the north, its dome catching the first grey light.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Perception (the wing imprint):** two shallow impressions in the glassy earth at the centre, the size of a man's knees. Aldric has knelt here every morning for months.
- **DC 13 Religion or Arcana:** the imprint radiates faint evocation and abjuration magic, and a lingering warmth. This is where Ilvaris first touched the earth in Gildenfield.
- **DC 14 Investigation (the circle's edge):** dozens of small offerings tucked into the wheat: wooden charms, ribbons, a child's drawing of a tall man in white. The town remembers the stranger kindly.
- **Captain:** if Captain is brought into the circle, it walks straight to the wing imprint, lies down at its centre, and refuses to move until Rook picks it up.

> [!tip] DM Note
> This is the quietest, most important scene of the two-parter. Keep it hushed: dew, birdsong just beginning, a frightened old man kneeling in a circle. The rising sun is the scene's clock: describe the light creeping down the hillside toward the field with each exchange. If [[The Son Turns]] runs here, the hollow under the old oak is the nearest darkness.
