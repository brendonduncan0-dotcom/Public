**Role:** Neutral (moral test, the glowing son)
**First appears:** Act 2
**Location:** [[Fennimore Cottage]]

## Personality
- Gentle, self-deprecating and determined not to be a burden; apologises for glowing
- Brave in the quiet way of someone who did the right thing without thinking
- Drifting: mid-sentence, he sometimes stops and turns his head toward the temple, as if he hears singing

## Appearance
A lanky twenty-year-old farmhand whose peeling, blistered skin gives off a soft pearly glow in the dark, like moonlight through milk.

## Motivation
Tam wants to stop being afraid and stop scaring his father. He'd like to see the sun again without it killing him. He saved a neighbour's child at noon a fortnight ago and lost six points of Constitution doing it; he's been fading ever since.

**Symptoms (describe, don't list):** blistered, peeling skin; unquenchable thirst (a bucket of water by the bed, nearly empty); no sweat despite the heat; milky-white eyes in bright light; a steady glow in darkness.

**Treating Tam:** applying [[Mara Thatch]]'s remedy (cellar mud, cold ash, nightshade paste) and making a DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check stabilises him. He stops fading, his glow dims, and he can sleep. On a failure, the remedy eases him for the night but he'll need more care in [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]]. Rook's Lesser Restoration or other magical healing can also stabilise him for the night (but doesn't cure him).

## Secret
In the afternoons, when Father Aldric knocks, Tam hears singing through the door and *wants* to let him in. He knows it isn't right and he's frightened of himself.

**In [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]]**, an untreated Tam is close to dying and rising as a glarewight; a treated Tam is stable and becomes an ally. [[Captain]] is gentle with him either way, a hint that he can be saved.
