> [!info] Read Aloud
> A tall, pale stone sanctum under a great dome of stained lens-glass, so bright at noon it hurts to look anywhere. Three blazing shafts of sunlight fall from the dome and cross on the altar, where a reliquary of mirrors and silver throws light back out in needles. Long saffron banners hang from a narrow gallery along the east and west walls. Rows of pews face the altar. On the floor before it, the stone is glazed pale and glassy in the shape of two great outspread wings.

## Notable Features

- **The dome:** a great dome of stained lens-glass with three large lens-windows (east, west, and south above the doors) that focus noon light into blazing shafts.
- **The banners:** two long saffron banners hanging beneath the east and west lens-windows, tied off on the gallery rails.
- **The gallery:** a narrow wooden balcony along the east and west walls, reached by spiral stairs in the north-east and north-west corners.
- **The lantern stands:** two tall brass stands of mirror-lanterns flanking the altar (north-east and north-west).
- **The reliquary:** on the altar, a cage of silver, mirrors and lenses around a cracked crystal that pulses with light.
- **The pews:** a dozen rows of oak pews, bleached silver-white and paper-light on their sunward sides.
- **The wing imprint:** on the floor before the altar steps, pale glazed stone in the shape of two outspread wings.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Perception:** the banner ropes are tied off to cleats on the gallery rail; one cut drops a banner across its window's sunbeam.
- **DC 13 Investigation (the lens-windows):** the lenses are what focus the light into blazing shafts; smash one and the light through that window scatters into ordinary daylight, dim in this big stone hall.
- **DC 14 Arcana or Detect Magic:** the reliquary radiates strong abjuration and evocation; the floor holds a lingering Hallow effect; the wing imprint shows faint evocation.
- **DC 15 Religion:** the stained glass shows the dawn of Lathander, but someone has repainted the faces of the figures in the glass so they all look upward at a tall figure in white.

## The Light Level Track

The sanctum's **Light Level** is the number of active light sources. It starts at **6** in Phase 1.

| # | Source | Ways to snuff it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | East lens-window | Cut the east banner (gallery action, or ranged attack AC 12 on the rope); smash the lens (AC 13, 10 HP); pitch smoke pot (2 rounds); Darkness or Fog Cloud |
| 2 | West lens-window | As east |
| 3 | South lens-window | Smash the lens, smoke pot, Darkness or Fog Cloud (no banner) |
| 4 | North-east lantern stand | Snuff (action, adjacent), knock over (DC 12 Athletics), smash (AC 11, 5 HP), or soot cloth |
| 5 | North-west lantern stand | As north-east |
| 6 | Reliquary mirror array | Cover with a cloth (DC 13 Sleight of Hand, or automatic with a soot cloth) or Darkness; can't be destroyed in Phase 1 |

- **Lit area:** at Light Level 4+, the whole sanctum is bright light. At 2–3, the pews and gallery are dim; only the shafts and the altar are bright. At 0–1, the sanctum is dark except within 10 feet of any remaining source.
- **Phase 2:** source 6 is gone (the reliquary shatters) but the Relic itself counts as 1 permanent source. The Relic can create new sources with Tear the Dome.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The sanctum was glimpsed briefly in [[Oneshot 4 - Sunblind]] (at noon service or through the vestry door), but this is the first time the party fights here. Keep the Light Level on the table as a die everyone can see. Describe the room getting darker as a *relief*: cool, quiet, safe. And make sure Captain ends up sitting on the wing imprint at some point, unwilling to move.
