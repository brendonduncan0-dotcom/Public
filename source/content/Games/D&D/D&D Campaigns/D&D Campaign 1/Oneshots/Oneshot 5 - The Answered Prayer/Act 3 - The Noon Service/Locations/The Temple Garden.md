> [!info] Read Aloud
> A formal temple garden climbs the hill in terraces toward the white dome, its gravel paths blazing under the noon sun. Mirrors are everywhere: on posts, on arches, set into the rims of still stone basins, all angled to throw light into every corner. There are no shadows. The flowers in the beds all face the temple doors, and the air shimmers with heat.

## Notable Features

- **The central path:** a straight gravel path running north up the hill to the temple's deep porch and open double doors.
- **Mirror arches:** three wooden arches over the path (south, centre, north), each hung with mirrors and lanterns.
- **Mirror basins:** shallow round stone basins of still water set along the path, their rims lined with mirror-glass.
- **Flower beds:** terraced beds of enormous, perfect flowers, all facing the temple doors.
- **The porch:** a deep, columned porch at the top of the path; the only shade in the garden.
- **The bell tower:** a slender bell tower on the east side of the temple, its bell still swaying.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 13 Perception (each arch):** a fine tripwire across the path beneath it, linked to the arch's mirror-lanterns. (Advantage if the party scouted the temple.)
- **DC 12 Investigation (the basins):** the basins are angled to throw reflected light under the porch roof. If two basins are drained or covered, the porch becomes dark.
- **DC 14 Perception:** heat-haze ripples drifting between the basins: two glarewights, late for service.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The garden is designed to have no shade, so the party must bring their own: smoke pots, Darkness, a soot cloth held between two people. Keep this fight short and use it as a warm-up for the light-control mechanics of the sanctum. Captain walking in through the garden on its own is the emotional beat to land here.
