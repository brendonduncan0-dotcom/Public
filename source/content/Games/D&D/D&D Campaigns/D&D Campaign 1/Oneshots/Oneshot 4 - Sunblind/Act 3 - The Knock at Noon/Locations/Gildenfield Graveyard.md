> [!info] Read Aloud
> A walled graveyard on a gentle slope beside the road, crowded far past what a town this size should need. In the old section to the north, mossy headstones lean under ancient yews. In the new section to the south, raw earth mounds stand in rows, black cloth knotted around their markers and stirring in the night breeze. The air smells of turned soil, and very faintly of hot stone.

## Notable Features

- **The old section (north):** mossy stones from the bad winter, iron nails hammered into the tops of the coffin-shaped grave slabs, salt lines along the old graves, and carved warnings: *BURY THEM FACE DOWN.*
- **The new section (south):** two dozen fresh mounds since midsummer, black cloth tied to every marker.
- **Burst graves:** seven of the new graves are broken open from inside, the earth around their rims baked pale and glassy as if fired in a kiln.
- **Osric's hut:** a tool shed by the gate with spades, a barrow, buckets of cellar mud and a stack of black cloth.
- **The yews:** three ancient yew trees in the old section, their shade deep even in daylight.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Investigation (burst graves):** every burst grave is marked on a little wooden tag with the time of burial, and every one of them says "day" or "noon." None of the night burials have burst.
- **DC 13 Religion (old section):** the wards here (nails, salt, face-down burial) are folk protections against undead, very different from the new rite. Whatever is happening now isn't the same thing as the bad winter.
- **DC 14 Perception (old section, at night):** a faint pearly glow seeping from the soil of one of the older new-section graves (see the complication in [[Before Dawn]]).
- **DC 15 Investigation (Corwin's grave):** a burst grave near the south wall with a carved marker, *CORWIN THATCH, TRAPPER, BELOVED*, and a bunch of fresh wildflowers on it. Someone visits often.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The graveyard is seen in passing from the road in Act 1; it is visited properly for the burial in Act 3. Keep it in total darkness during [[Before Dawn]]: the whole point is that the party must work without light. If Wendel rises, dawn light comes over the eastern wall: dim for the first 2 rounds, then bright.
