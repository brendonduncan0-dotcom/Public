**Act:** 3 — [[Act 3 - The Noon Service]]
**Location:** [[The Sun Temple]]
**Encounter type:** Combat (Boss Phase 2)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The thing above the altar turns its face toward you, and for a heartbeat it is almost kind, almost a tall stranger in white with a voice like warm bread. Then the face slides away into glare. The snuffed lanterns beside the altar flicker and catch again by themselves. Above, with a sound like ice cracking on a pond, the dome begins to split, and a new spear of noon stabs down through the gap. The light is *hungry*. And it has seen the cat.

## Setup
Phase 2 of the finale. The caged deva-light has been released from Aldric and twisted into a hunger for eternal noon. It wants the sanctum blazing, and it wants Captain's light most of all. The **Light Level** track continues from Phase 1 (the Relic itself counts as 1 source that can't be snuffed).

**What changes from Phase 1:**
- The Relic relights lanterns (Kindle, bonus action, every turn) and can **Tear the Dome** to create new sunbeams (new sources). A torn dome shaft can only be blocked by smoke, magical darkness, Fog Cloud, or a cloth thrown over the opening from the gallery (an action on the gallery, DC 12 Dexterity check).
- Any glarewights left over from Phase 1 keep fighting.
- **Soot-blackened weapons** must be re-coated (one use, an action) to count as magical against the Relic for this phase, unless they were re-coated during the transition.
- **Necrotic damage** is the party's best friend: the Relic is vulnerable to it. Osric's Black Shovel shines here.

**Protect Captain.** Each turn, the Relic tries to Draw the Light from Captain if Captain is in bright light within 60 feet. Captain can be protected by:
- Rook (or anyone) carrying Captain under a cloak or in a pack (the cat counts as in darkness).
- Keeping Captain inside smoke, Darkness, or the dark gallery.
- Captain won't flee the sanctum, whatever anyone does.

After the third Draw, Captain goes limp and dark: Rook feels his patron's presence vanish like a door closing. This is **not death**: Captain revives the moment the Relic is destroyed, but Rook loses access to his patron's support (his warlock features feel cold and distant, as flavour; use your judgement) until then. It's a very strong motivator.

## Objectives
- Keep the sanctum dark.
- Destroy the Relic of Ilvaris.
- Protect Captain.

| [[Glarewight]] | 1 | 0–2 | Any survivors from Phase 1 |

**Terrain features:** See [[The Sun Temple]]. The dome now tears open; the pews begin to bleach and splinter wherever the Relic passes.

> [!tip] DM Note: The Payoff
> Everything the party gathered across both one-shots should matter here: smoke pots blocking new dome shafts, soot cloths smothering relit lanterns, the Darkness scroll on the worst sunbeam, flour (useless against the huge, visible Relic, but still revealing any leftover glarewights), Osric's shovel, Mara's lessons. Call out each one as it lands. If **Aldric is alive and conscious** (talked down after being reached), he can act **once**: he staggers up, throws his arms around the Relic's light, and holds it, *"Hold it shut, hold it shut"*, and the Relic is **Restrained until the end of its next turn**. Then he collapses for good.

> [!warning] Scaling
> - **Party struggling:** The Relic forgets Captain for a round, entranced by its own reflection in the broken mirrors; or Hollis/Mara bursts in and smothers a lantern stand.
> - **Party cruising:** The Relic uses Tear the Dome every round the Light Level is 3 or lower, not just at 2.
> - **Retreat:** The Relic is Anchored to the sanctum. The party can always retreat into the garden. The Relic hovers over the altar and waits; the party can return after a short rest, but it regains 30 HP.

## Possible Outcomes
- **The Relic falls:** It collapses inward, shrinking, folding, until all that's left is the cracked crystal on the shattered reliquary, pulsing with a faint light like a heartbeat. The sanctum is suddenly, wonderfully quiet. Captain, if it was drawn, lifts its head and sneezes. Run [[The Choice]].
- **The party retreats:** See above. The town keeps hiding; the contract isn't complete until the Relic falls.
