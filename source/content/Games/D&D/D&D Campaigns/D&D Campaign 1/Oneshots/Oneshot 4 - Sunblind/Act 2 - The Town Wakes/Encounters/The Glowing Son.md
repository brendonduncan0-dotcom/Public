**Encounter type:** Social

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The Fennimore cottage is the only house on the lane that didn't open at dusk. Its door is still barred, the shutters nailed. As you come closer you hear a man's voice inside, low and steady, the way you'd talk to a frightened horse. Then, through a crack in the shutters, you see it: a soft, pearly glow, like moonlight through milk, coming from somewhere inside.

## Setup
[[Garrick Fennimore]] has barricaded himself and his son [[Tam Fennimore]] inside. Tam has lost six points of Constitution to repeated glarewight touches and is fading. Garrick won't open the door to anyone, terrified the town will take Tam away or that the priest will come knocking and Tam will let him in.

This is the session's moral test. It can't be won by force: forcing the door means Garrick fights to protect his son, Tam panics, and the family never trusts the party again.

## Objectives
- Get Garrick to open the door.
- Treat Tam (and learn more about sun sickness in the process).

## NPC Goals
- **Garrick:** protect his son from everyone. He'll open up only for someone who convinces him they can *help*, not just look.
- **Tam:** be useful; stop scaring his father. He'll talk to the party through the door if they speak to him directly, and he can persuade his father.

## Skill Challenge

| Check | DC | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persuasion (to Garrick, through the door) | 15 (12 with Town Trust 2+, or if the party has Mara's remedy) | Garrick unbars the door. | "Go away." Try again with a new approach. |
| Talk to Tam directly (any Charisma check) | 12 | Tam asks his father to let them in. Garrick relents. | Tam goes quiet; Garrick tells them to leave. |
| [[Captain]] at the door | — | Captain scratches at the door and mews. Tam laughs for the first time in weeks: "Da, there's a *cat*." Advantage on the next check. | |
| Medicine (treating Tam with the remedy) | 13 | Tam stabilises: his glow dims, he drinks, he sleeps. Town Trust +1. | Eased for the night, but he'll need more care in [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]]. |
| Medicine or Insight (examining Tam) | 12 | The touches are worse than sunburn: his body is slowly *filling with light*. Untreated, it'll take him within a few days, and then he'll rise. | |

Rook's Lesser Restoration, or similar magical healing, also counts as treatment for tonight.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Door opened and Tam treated:** Garrick breaks down, then shakes every hand. Tam thanks them sleepily, then says something odd: "When the priest knocks, it sounds like singing. I wanted to open the door. I'm sorry." Captain curls up on Tam's chest and purrs. (A hint that Tam can be saved, and a clue that the light is *calling* to itself.)
- **Door opened, Tam not treated:** Garrick lets them see Tam but asks them to leave by midnight. Tam's condition carries into [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]], where he's close to turning.
- **Door forced:** Garrick fights (see his stat block) and Tam cowers, glowing, in the corner, begging them to stop. Town Trust −2, and the Fennimores won't trust the party in Oneshot 5. Don't let this become a real fight: Garrick surrenders after one blow, sobbing.
