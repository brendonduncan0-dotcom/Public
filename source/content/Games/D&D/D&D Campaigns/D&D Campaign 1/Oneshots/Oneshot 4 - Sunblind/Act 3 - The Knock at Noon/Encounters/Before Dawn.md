**Act:** 3 — [[Act 3 - The Knock at Noon]]
**Location:** [[Gildenfield Graveyard]]
**Encounter type:** Exploration (skill challenge; combat if it fails)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The graveyard is black and silent, the sky above the eastern hills just beginning to turn the colour of a bruise. Osric sets down his lantern, looks at you, and blows it out. In the sudden total dark, his voice is very close. "No light from here on. Not a candle, not a spark. Dig by feel. And be quick about it: dawn doesn't wait for anyone. I've learned that the hard way. Seven times."

## Setup
Wendel Pike died yesterday morning in the sun. Unless he is buried properly **before dawn**, he will rise as a glarewight at sunrise. The rite, as [[Osric Loam]] teaches it, has four requirements:
1. **Before dawn.**
2. **In total darkness**: no light of any kind, magical or mundane.
3. **Eyes wrapped in soot-blackened cloth** (one use of Mara's soot kit, or lamp-black from the cooperage or the Chandler's cellar).
4. **Body packed in cellar mud** (from The Late Supper's cellar, or any cellar in town).

If the party never learned the rite or left Wendel's body unattended, skip to *Wendel rises* below.

## Objectives
- Complete the burial before dawn, in total darkness.
- Optional: help Osric re-secure an older grave that's starting to glow.

## Skill Challenge

The party needs **4 successes before 3 failures**. Each PC should contribute at least one check. Darkvision helps (Rindle sees in the dark; others are working blind). Osric gives Advantage on one check of the party's choice.

| Check | DC | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Athletics (dig the grave fast, in the dark) | 12 | Six feet down, square and true. | Slow going; the sky lightens. |
| Dexterity or Sleight of Hand (wrap the eyes in soot cloth, by touch) | 11 | Wrapped tight. | Fumbled; the cloth slips, and someone must re-wrap it. |
| Survival or Nature (pack the mud properly) | 12 | Packed thick and cold. | Gaps; Osric curses and fixes it, costing time. |
| Religion or Performance (a rite or a few words for Wendel) | 10 | Osric nods: "That'll do. He'd have liked that." | Awkward, but heartfelt. Counts as a failure only if no one tries. |
| Perception (watch for light: a lantern in town, a glowing PC) | 13 | A PC with sun sickness (3+ Con lost) is glowing faintly; spotted and covered in time. | An unnoticed glow breaks the darkness; Osric hisses, "*Cover that!*" |
| Medicine (lay the body right) | 10 | Laid face-up, arms crossed, as Osric wants. | |

**Complication (optional, for tension):** halfway through, a faint pearly glow seeps up through the soil of an older grave nearby: one of Osric's failed burials, stirring. A PC can spend a check (Athletics DC 12, with mud and Osric's help) to re-pack it. If ignored, nothing happens tonight, but it's a reminder of what failure looks like.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Success:** Wendel stays down. The sun rises over a quiet graveyard. Osric leans on his shovel for a long moment, then holds it out: *"Lend you this. Till it's done."* The party gets the **Black Shovel** (see [[Osric Loam]]). Town Trust +1.
- **Wendel rises (3 failures, or the rite wasn't done):** As the first light hits the graveyard, the earth over Wendel's grave (or wherever his body lies) bakes pale and glassy and bursts. A new glarewight rises, eyeless and shining. The dawn light is **dim** for the first 2 rounds, then bright. Fight it, or fall back to darkness. Osric still lends the shovel if the party fights beside him. If the party wasn't here at all, Wendel's glarewight joins the attack on the mill in [[Siege of Dunmore's Mill]] as a fifth enemy.

> [!warning] Scaling
> One fresh glarewight is a light fight after a long night. If the party is exhausted (low on slots and HP), Osric fights beside them with the shovel, or the glarewight is "newborn" and has only 12 HP. If the party is fresh, let the burst grave next door rise too (2 glarewights).
