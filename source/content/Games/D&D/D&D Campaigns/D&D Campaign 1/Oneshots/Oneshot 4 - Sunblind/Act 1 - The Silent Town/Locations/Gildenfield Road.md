> [!info] Read Aloud
> The road into Gildenfield runs between fields of wheat so tall and golden they look painted, heavy-headed and rustling though there's no wind. The flowers in the verges all face the same way: not toward the sun, but toward a white temple dome on the hill above town. Past a low graveyard crowded with fresh mounds, the high street opens up, every door barred and every window painted black. It's silent but for the buzz of fat bees and the faint creak of a lantern swinging on its bracket, lit, at noon.

## Notable Features

- **Too-perfect fields:** wheat taller than a person, no weeds, no blight; the crop is weeks ahead of season.
- **Flowers facing the temple:** every poppy, daisy and dandelion turned toward the dome on the hill, regardless of where the sun is.
- **The graveyard on the left of the road:** dozens of fresh mounds; black cloth tied to many headstones.
- **Painted windows:** every pane on the high street blacked out with paint or soot from the inside.
- **Lit lanterns:** iron lanterns on brackets every few yards down the high street, all burning in broad daylight.
- **A dropped hat:** Hobb's straw hat, lying in the road where his horse threw him.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Perception (at the graveyard):** several graves are burst open *from inside*, the earth around them baked pale and glassy at the edges, as if fired in a kiln.
- **DC 13 Investigation (any well or trough):** the water is warm, warmer than the day.
- **DC 13 Nature:** there has been no frost here this year, not even in early spring, and the crops have grown far faster than they should. Something is making it summer.
- **DC 15 Perception (on the high street):** a faint scratching from behind a barred door, then a whisper: "Don't stand in the sun. *Please* don't stand in the sun." (A villager. They won't open.)
- **Detect Magic:** a faint evocation aura over the whole valley, strongest at the temple on the hill.

> [!tip] DM Note
> This is the party's first impression of Gildenfield, and it should feel like a beautiful summer postcard with something badly wrong in every corner. Let the players poke around. The graveyard is glimpsed here from the road; it is explored properly in [[Gildenfield Graveyard]] in Act 3. Aldric is met here (see [[The Lantern-Lighter]]). The temple on the hill is visible but should not be explored in detail in this adventure.
