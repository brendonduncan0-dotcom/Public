The party takes Gildenfield's pooled contract and returns at dusk to a town that has turned its biggest cellar into a war room. This act is about preparation and community: the town gives the party everything it has (soot, smoke, flour, black cloth, a remedy for Tam) and the party spends the night getting ready for noon. At midnight, Hollis Wick's well-meaning mirror rig floods the night market with light and draws the glarewights into the crowd, and the party gets to save the town in front of everyone. By the end of the act, the party should be armed for the finale and on their way to meet Aldric at dawn.
