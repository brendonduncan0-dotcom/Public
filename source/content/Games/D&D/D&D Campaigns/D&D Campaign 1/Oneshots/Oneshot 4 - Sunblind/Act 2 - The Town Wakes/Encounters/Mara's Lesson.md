**Act:** 2 — [[Act 2 - The Town Wakes]]
**Location:** [[Mara's House]]
**Encounter type:** Social

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Mara's house is less a home than a workshop for killing things. Snares hang drying from the rafters. Jars of lamp-black and pitch crowd the shelves. Half the floor is dusted with flour, raked into neat rows like a garden, and every footprint shows. She doesn't look up from the crossbow bolt she's blackening with a rag. "You've got questions," she says. "I've got work. Make yourself useful and I'll answer some."

## Setup
[[Mara Thatch]] is preparing for another day of hiding. She knows more than anyone in town about fighting the glarewights, worked out through grief and trial and error. She'll share it, but she wants to see the party do something useful first. With Town Trust 2 or more she skips this and starts teaching.

**If the party broke into her house by day:** she was behind a barricade with a crossbow, a bear trap and caltrops. She stopped shooting only when someone bled and cast a shadow. She's cold (Trust −1 for her personally) and needs a DC 15 Persuasion or a genuine apology plus help with her work before she'll teach.

## Objectives
- Earn Mara's trust.
- Learn the soot-blade technique (and get her soot kit).
- Learn the sunburn remedy.
- Optional: learn Mara's secret about Corwin.

## NPC Goals
Mara wants the party to prove they're useful, not just brave. She wants the thing that killed Corwin gone. Deep down, she wants someone to tell her it wasn't her fault.

## Skill Challenge

Give Mara a task and let the party solve it; any reasonable approach works.

| Task | Check | DC | Success |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reset the bear traps along the fence line | Thieves' tools or Survival | 12 | Mara: "Huh. Not useless." |
| Blacken a batch of bolts and staves | Alchemist's supplies or Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) | 10 | She watches, corrects their grip, approves. |
| Carry water from the well for the remedy | Athletics | 8 | It's heavy work, and she notices who does it without being asked. |
| Talk her round instead | Persuasion | 14 | Grudging respect, and she makes them blacken the bolts anyway. |

**Once she's won over, Mara teaches:**
1. **Soot-blades:** coat a weapon in soot or lamp-black pitch, and it hurts the sun-ghosts "like a real blade should" for one fight. She hands over **Mara's Soot Kit** (6 uses; see [[Mara Thatch]]). **This is the payoff moment for the Act 1 ambush: make it feel like being handed the answer.** Rindle can make more (2 uses per hour, DC 12 Intelligence check with alchemist's supplies).
2. **The remedy:** cellar mud, cold ash and nightshade paste, applied at night, restores `dice: 1d4` lost Constitution. She has enough for one application per PC tonight, and Rindle can make more with the recipe.
3. **Her rules:** "Don't fight them in the sun. Drag them into the dark. Throw flour, it sticks. And for gods' sake, don't let Hollis Wick light anything near you."

## Possible Outcomes
- **Trust earned:** Soot kit, remedy, and Mara's advice. Town Trust +1. She'll fight beside the party if they're in trouble near her house.
- **The party asks about her husband:** DC 14 Insight or gentle Persuasion: she admits Corwin died at noon checking snares, and she buried him in daylight because she couldn't bear to wait. He rose. "He's one of them now. I see the ring sometimes. In the sun, when the light catches it." (A tin wedding ring.) **Bonus objective:** lay Corwin to rest; see [[Siege of Dunmore's Mill]].
- **Mara is cold (low trust):** She tosses them a single use of soot and says "you'll learn the rest the hard way." The remedy recipe can be found in [[The Chandler's Cellar]] or learned from the Fennimores.
