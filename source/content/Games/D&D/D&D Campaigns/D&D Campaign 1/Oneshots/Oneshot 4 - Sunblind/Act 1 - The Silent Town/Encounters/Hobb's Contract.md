**Act:** 1 — [[Act 1 - The Silent Town]]
**Location:** [[The Guild Headquarters]]
**Encounter type:** Social

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The contracts officer slides a sheet across the desk, then nods toward the halfling hovering by the door, who is holding an empty grain sack in both hands as if it might escape. "Client's here in person," she says. "He insisted. He's been here since dawn." The halfling takes a deep breath, opens his mouth, and says: "Before anything else, I need you to know that I am not mad. I'm sweating, but I'm not mad."

## Setup
[[Hobb Barleycorn]] has filed a contract and refused to leave until he could explain it in person. This scene is short (10–15 minutes) and exists to deliver the contract, set up the eerie details of Gildenfield, and get the first laugh of the night. Hand the players the contract text from Hobb's NPC note.

## Objectives
- Accept the contract and learn the terms (350 gp on delivery of the grain, +100 gp bonus for a full account of the disturbance).
- Learn what Hobb saw in Gildenfield.
- Optionally, negotiate expenses or an advance.

## NPC Goals
Hobb wants someone to take him seriously, find Wendel, and get his grain. He'll answer every question at length and slightly out of order.

## Skill Challenge

| Check | DC | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insight | 10 | Hobb is telling the truth, but he's embarrassed about something (he hid in a barrel until dusk; see his Secret). | He seems honest and very frightened. |
| Persuasion | 13 | Hobb advances 50 gp of the reward for supplies ("Lamp oil? Mirrors? I don't know what you need for *this*!"). | He can't spare an advance but gives the party a spare grain sack, "just in case." |
| Persuasion or Intimidation (after Insight success) | 12 | Hobb admits he stayed till dusk and heard the town *come out* at night: "Doors opening, people talking, *a market*. At midnight!" | He changes the subject to Wendel. |

## Possible Outcomes
- **Contract accepted:** The party sets out; Gildenfield is half a day's ride, so they arrive around midday. Run [[The Lantern-Lighter]].
- **Hobb admits the barrel:** The party knows the town wakes at night, which can save them from forcing doors in Act 1.
- **Levity beat:** If anyone asks why he's holding the sack, Hobb says, "In case I need to breathe into it," and does so.
