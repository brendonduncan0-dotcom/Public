> [!info] Read Aloud
> Gildenfield's market square by moonlight: a cobbled open space ringed with timber-framed shops, a stone well at its centre, and a little wooden bandstand on the north side. Market stalls crowd the cobbles, heaped with glossy, oversized vegetables. At each corner of the square stands a tall post hung with lanterns and angled mirrors, unlit for now, all pointing inward.

## Notable Features

- **The well:** a waist-high round stone well in the centre of the square, with a bucket and winch.
- **The bandstand:** a raised wooden platform on the north side, with a rail and a big iron lever bolted to the floor.
- **Market stalls:** a dozen canvas-topped stalls selling oversized vegetables, cheeses, candles and soot.
- **Rig posts:** four tall timber posts at the north, east, south and west edges, each hung with four lanterns behind angled mirrors, linked by wires running along the shopfronts to the bandstand lever.
- **Dark alleys:** narrow lanes leading out of each corner of the square, unlit.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Perception (before the flare):** wires strung along the shopfront eaves all lead to the bandstand. Something has been rigged.
- **DC 13 Investigation (the bandstand lever):** freshly oiled, recently installed; the work of someone who knows mirrors and lenses. Hollis.
- **DC 14 Perception (during the flare):** the temple windows on the hill pulse in time with the square's lanterns. The light is *calling*.

> [!tip] DM Note
> Lantern Square is where Gildenfield gathers every night, and the place the party most visibly saves the town. Make the crowd vivid: names, faces, a child dropping a toy. The four rig posts are the battlefield's "light switches," and the dark alleys are safe havens the party can herd people into.
