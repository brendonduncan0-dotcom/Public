**Role:** Quest giver (client)
**First appears:** Act 1
**Location:** [[The Guild Headquarters]]

## Personality
- A champion worrier who narrates his own anxiety ("I'm sweating. Am I sweating? I'm sweating.")
- Fundamentally decent: he is genuinely more upset about his missing carter than his missing grain, though he'd like both back
- Prone to over-preparing in useless ways; he arrives with a list, a spare list, and a pencil for each

## Appearance
A round, pink-cheeked halfling in an oversized straw hat, with a grain sack slung over one shoulder that he never puts down and that is, he insists, "just in case."

## Motivation
Hobb paid Dunmore's Mill in full for a season's grain order, and his business depends on it. He sent his carter Wendel Pike ahead with a wagon two days ago; Wendel never came back. Hobb went himself yesterday, arrived at midday, found every door barred, watched a priest light lanterns in broad daylight, and fled when his horse refused to go near the square. He wants the grain, he wants Wendel found, and he wants someone to tell him he isn't going mad.

**What he tells the party:**
- He arrived at midday to find every door barred and windows painted black.
- A man in priest's robes was lighting lanterns in broad daylight; he smiled and waved.
- His horse refused to go near the square and nearly threw him.
- On the road in, he saw far too many fresh graves, "and some of them looked dug *out*, not dug *in*."
- Wendel's wagon was nowhere in sight.

## Secret
Hobb never actually left Gildenfield on time: he hid in an empty rain barrel on the edge of town until dusk, heard the town wake up, and panicked and ran before anyone spotted him. He's embarrassed about it. If the party mentions the night market, he blurts out "the people come *out* at night?" and then has to explain. He'll turn up at the end of Act 3 with a new wagon, having found his nerve (see [[Dusk at the Mill Door]]).

## The Contract

> **Guild Contract — Faerûn Adventure League**
> **Client:** Hobb Barleycorn, Barleycorn & Daughters Grain Merchants, Waterdeep
> **Objective:** Collect a paid grain order (forty sacks) from Dunmore's Mill, Gildenfield, and return it to Waterdeep. Establish the cause of the disturbance in Gildenfield and report.
> **Reward:** 350 gp on delivery of the grain. 100 gp bonus for a full and credible account of the disturbance.
> **Note in the margin, underlined twice:** *Please find Wendel.*
