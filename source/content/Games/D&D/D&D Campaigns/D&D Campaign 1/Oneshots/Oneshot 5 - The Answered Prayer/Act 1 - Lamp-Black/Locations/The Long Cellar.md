> [!info] Read Aloud
> A long barrel-vaulted cellar under the old tithe barn, cool and smelling of earth and apples. Straw pallets line the walls where half the town sleeps by day. Tonight it's crowded with trestle tables piled with flour sacks, pots of pitch, bundles of black cloth and crates of lamp-black, all lit by a single shuttered lantern. On the end wall, someone has chalked a big, careful map of the temple on the hill.

## Notable Features

- **The chalk map:** a detailed drawing of the temple, garden and sanctum on the north wall, annotated in Mara's tidy hand and Pip's enthusiastic one ("BRIGHT THING HERE!!").
- **Supply tables:** flour sacks, pitch pots, rags, lamp-black, buckets of cellar mud, and a heap of black cloth.
- **Straw pallets:** dozens of them along both long walls, where townsfolk sleep through the day.
- **The single lantern:** a shuttered tin lantern hung from the central beam, turned down to a glow.
- **Tam's corner:** a pallet at the far end, curtained off with sacking, where [[Tam Fennimore]] rests if he's here.
- **A barrel of apples:** enormous, flawless apples from the too-perfect orchards.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Investigation (the chalk map):** Pip has drawn six little suns in the sanctum, three in the dome, two on stands, one on the altar. She's counted the temple's light sources better than any adult.
- **DC 13 Perception:** Osric has quietly stacked six coffins' worth of black cloth by the stairs. He's planning for the worst.
- **DC 10 Insight (the townsfolk):** they're terrified, but this is the first time in months they've had something to *do*. Morale is high.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The Long Cellar is the party's base for all of Oneshot 5: safe, dark, full of allies. Use it to show how much the town has changed toward the party. If Town Trust from Oneshot 4 was low, the cellar is quieter and only Mara and Osric show up; if it was high, it's packed. The party can return here for a short rest between Act 2 and Act 3.
