> [!info] Read Aloud
> A cramped stone room that smells of lamp oil, candle wax and hot metal. Saffron robes hang on pegs. A writing desk stands under the window, buried in papers, with lens-grinding tools laid out neatly beside a leather-bound book. Under the heavy door in the north wall, a line of brilliant white light leaks from the sanctum beyond, and from behind it comes a soft, constant humming.

## Notable Features

- **The writing desk:** papers, quills, a half-burned candle, lens-grinding tools and Aldric's leather-bound journal.
- **Robes on pegs:** faded saffron vestments of a priest of Lathander.
- **The sanctum door:** heavy oak, iron-bound, unlocked, with blinding light leaking under it and humming beyond.
- **Mirror sketches pinned to the wall:** dozens of diagrams of angled mirrors and lenses converging on a single point.
- **A narrow cot:** made up with military neatness and clearly never slept in.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 13 Perception:** two fine tripwires across the floor (one by the outer door, one in front of the desk), each running to a lantern-and-mirror rig in a corner.
- **DC 13 Investigation (desk drawer):** a bundle of letters from the Waterdeep temple of Lathander asking why Gildenfield's tithes have stopped, all unopened.
- **DC 15 Investigation (behind the sketches):** a final sketch, different from the rest: the reliquary drawn whole, with a crack across the crystal, and in the margin, in Aldric's hand, *"Hold it shut. Hold it shut. Hold it shut."*
- **DC 12 Perception (the cot):** the blanket has been bleached bone-white in the exact shape of a man lying down, but the rest of the cot is untouched.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The vestry is the only part of the temple the party should enter in this adventure; the sanctum beyond the door, with its stained-glass dome and the reliquary, is saved for the finale of [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]]. If players try to open the sanctum door, describe a wall of light so bright it hurts, a shape kneeling in the middle of it, and the humming stopping. Then Captain yowls from outside, and the light seems to turn its attention toward the sound. Let them choose to close the door.
