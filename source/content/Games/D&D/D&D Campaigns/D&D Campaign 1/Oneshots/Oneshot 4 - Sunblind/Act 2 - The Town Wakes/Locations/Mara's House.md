> [!info] Read Aloud
> A trapper's cottage at the edge of the fields, more workshop than home. Snares and coils of wire hang drying from the rafters. Shelves sag under jars of lamp-black, pitch and something that smells like a swamp. The floor is dusted white with flour, raked into neat furrows, and every footprint stands out like a signature.

## Notable Features

- **The flour floor:** raked flour across the whole front room; any footprint shows instantly.
- **The barricade:** an overturned table and two sacks of earth facing the door, with a crossbow rest notched into the top.
- **Shelves of jars:** lamp-black, pitch, cellar mud, cold ash and dried nightshade, all labelled in a tidy hand.
- **Drying snares:** dozens of wire and cord snares hanging from the beams.
- **The workbench:** crossbow bolts half-blackened with soot, a rag, a pot of pitch.
- **A man's coat on a hook:** a big, patched oilskin coat that is obviously not Mara's size.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Perception (by the door):** a bear trap under a scattering of straw just inside the threshold, and caltrops scattered beneath the window. (Already sprung if the party broke in by day.)
- **DC 13 Investigation (workbench drawer):** a folded page torn from [[Osric Loam]]'s ledger: *"Corwin Thatch. Buried 2nd hr past noon by his widow's wish. Rose."* Mara has been hiding it.
- **DC 14 Insight (watching Mara):** she glances at the oilskin coat every time someone mentions the glarewights.

> [!tip] DM Note
> Mara's house is a working trapper's den, and every trap here is a lesson the party can take with them. If the party broke in by day, the bear trap and caltrops have been sprung and Mara starts behind the barricade with her crossbow (see her stat block); she stops shooting the moment someone bleeds and casts a shadow. Otherwise, this is a calm night scene: a workshop, a lesson, and a widow who needs to hear it wasn't her fault.
