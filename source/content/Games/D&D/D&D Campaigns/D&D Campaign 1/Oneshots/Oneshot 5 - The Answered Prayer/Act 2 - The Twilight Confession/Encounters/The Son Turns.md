**Act:** 2 — [[Act 2 - The Twilight Confession]]
**Location:** [[Ilvaris's Field]]
**Encounter type:** Combat (optional)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Something is moving through the wheat toward you, glowing softly in the grey light. It's Tam Fennimore, barefoot, in his nightshirt, a torn strip of linen still knotted round one wrist. His eyes are milky white and fixed on Aldric, and he's smiling the same serene smile. Far behind him, a man's voice is shouting his name. As the first edge of the sun touches the hills, Tam's skin begins to blaze.

## Setup
**Optional.** Run this only if [[Tam Fennimore]] was **not** treated in [[Oneshot 4 - Sunblind]] or in [[Preparing for Noon]]. Drawn by the light in Aldric, Tam slipped out of the Long Cellar before dawn and walked to the field. As the sun rises, he begins to turn into a glarewight.

**Tam isn't dead yet.** For this encounter, use the [[Glarewight]] stat block for Tam, with these changes:
- He won't attack his father, Garrick (who arrives running in round 2), and he hesitates: the first time each PC is targeted, Tam has Disadvantage on the attack.
- **Saving him:** if Tam is in **darkness** (a cloak or tarp thrown over him, a Darkness spell, the hollow under the old oak) at the start of any of his turns, he stops, gasping and human for a moment. While he's in darkness, a PC can apply Mara's remedy or cast Lesser Restoration (or any healing spell) on him. If they do, the light drains out of him into the earth and he collapses, alive and himself. Captain can help: if Captain is with Tam in darkness, the check or spell needs no roll.
- **If Tam is reduced to 0 hit points**, he dies, and the glarewight light leaves him in a flare. This is a real tragedy for the Fennimores; Osric buries him properly that night.

## Objectives
- Save Tam: get him into darkness and treat him.
- Avoid killing him.

## Enemies
| Creature | CR | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Glarewight]] | 1 | 1 | Tam, turning; won't attack his father; hesitates against each PC once |

**Terrain features:** See [[Ilvaris's Field]]. Dawn light is **dim** for the first 2 rounds, then **bright**. The hollow beneath the old oak on the east edge of the field is dark.

> [!warning] Scaling
> This is an emotional encounter, not a hard one. If the party is struggling, let Garrick throw his coat over Tam (darkness) in round 3. If Aldric was **reached** in [[The Twilight Confession]], he can make the ultimate gesture: he holds out his hands and says "Give it to me, son," and pulls the light out of Tam into himself. Tam is saved instantly, but **Aldric gains 20 extra hit points** at noon. It's his choice, and he makes it gladly.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Tam saved:** Garrick reaches his son and holds him, sobbing. Tam, exhausted and human, looks at the party: "I heard singing. I'm sorry." Town Trust +1. The Fennimores will be waiting outside the temple at noon to see it end.
- **Aldric saves Tam:** Garrick stares at the priest as the light pours into him. Aldric says only, "I owed this town one," and walks toward the temple as the sun rises. This should hit hard.
- **Tam dies:** The light flares out of him and is gone. Garrick doesn't speak to anyone. It's a heavy moment; don't linger on it, but let it matter in the ending.
