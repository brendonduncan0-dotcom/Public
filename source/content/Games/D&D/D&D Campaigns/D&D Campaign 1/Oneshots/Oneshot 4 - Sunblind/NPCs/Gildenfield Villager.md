## Tactics

These are frightened farmers, not fighters. They swing wildly at anything that comes through the door, hide behind furniture, and rely on their household traps (ball bearings, bell strings, a bucket of flour over the door). They never pursue intruders out into daylight. If one drops to 0 HP, the others immediately surrender, begging the "ghosts" to spare them. Encourage the party to knock them out rather than kill them: a killed villager is a disaster for town trust.
