**Act:** 1 — [[Act 1 - Lamp-Black]]
**Location:** [[The Long Cellar]]
**Encounter type:** Exploration (preparation)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Gildenfield has turned its biggest cellar into a war room. Under the low vaults, by the light of a single shuttered lantern, the town is waiting for you: Mara with a crate of lamp-black, Osric leaning on his shovel, Dorrie with a pot of something hot, and a dozen farmers who've brought every sack of flour, scrap of black cloth and pot of pitch in the valley. Someone has chalked a map of the temple on the wall. Mara taps it. "Noon tomorrow," she says. "Tell us what you need."

## Setup
The party has the night to prepare for the noon assault on the temple. This is a planning scene and a showcase for the town's gratitude. It should feel like the "gearing up" montage in a heist film.

The party has **5 Preparation Hours** before dawn (they must leave for [[Ilvaris's Field]] an hour before sunrise). Each preparation below costs 1 hour of one PC's time; PCs can work in parallel, so four PCs can each do one preparation in the same hour. **The night market flare ([[The Night Market Flare]]) interrupts at midnight, after the second hour.** A short rest costs 1 hour.

Track everything the party makes; each item has a specific use in [[Act 3 - The Noon Service]].

## Objectives
- Gather resources for the finale.
- Treat Tam, if he wasn't treated in [[Oneshot 4 - Sunblind]].
- Learn about the temple's layout and defences.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Well prepared:** The party heads into Act 3 with smoke, flour, cloth and soot. The finale should feel like a plan coming together.
- **Underprepared:** The finale is harder but still winnable. Weapons can still be blackened on the spot from Mara's kit, and the Light Level track still works with bare hands and actions.
- **Mara's offer:** If Town Trust is 2 or more, Mara insists on coming to the garden assault. Use her stat block from [[Mara Thatch]] as an ally; she stays outside the sanctum ("That's your job. I'll hold the door.").
- **Levity beat:** Dorrie's chalkboard: *"NOON IN: 14 HOURS. DRINK ACCORDINGLY."*
