> [!info] Read Aloud
> A low, warm common room under smoke-dark beams, crowded with farmers eating breakfast at midnight. Every candle burns inside a shuttered tin lantern, turned down to a glow. The air smells of stew, ale and wet wool. Over the bar, a chalkboard counts down the hours to sunrise.

## Notable Features

- **The painted-over sign:** outside, "The Early Riser" has been painted over in black with "The Late Supper" in Dorrie's handwriting.
- **The sunrise chalkboard:** "SUNRISE IN: 9 HOURS. DRINK ACCORDINGLY." Dorrie updates it every hour.
- **Shuttered tin lanterns:** every light source in the room is hooded and dim; nobody here trusts a bright flame.
- **Enormous vegetables:** stew full of glossy, perfect, oversized carrots and turnips from fields that grow too well.
- **Osric's corner:** a corner table where the gravedigger sits alone with his black shovel propped beside him.
- **The card table:** four farmers playing a game where everyone is visibly cheating.
- **The cellar hatch:** behind the bar, where the regulars sleep by day.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Perception:** several patrons have faint golden scars on their hands or faces, like gilt worked into old wounds. Healed, they say, "that summer the stranger walked the fields."
- **DC 14 Insight:** for all the laughter, half the room flinches every time a lantern flares.
- **DC 13 Investigation (behind the bar):** Pip's charcoal drawings pinned under the counter, dozens of them, all of the temple with a shining shape inside and a small figure in front of it with its arms spread.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The Late Supper is the party's safe haven for the whole two-parter: food, rest, rumours and allies. Glarewights never come here at night. The cellar is a secure place for a short rest (and a long rest, if the party spends the night). The lantern inspection happens on the street outside at dusk; the investigation hub happens inside.
