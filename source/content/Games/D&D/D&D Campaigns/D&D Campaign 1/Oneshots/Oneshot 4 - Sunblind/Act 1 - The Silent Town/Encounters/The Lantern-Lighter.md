**Location:** [[Gildenfield Road]]
**Encounter type:** Social

> [!info] Read Aloud
> At the head of the street, in the full blaze of noon, a man in saffron robes is standing on a stepladder, lighting a lantern. He takes his time over it, trimming the wick, adjusting the glass, until the little flame is burning uselessly against the sun. Then he climbs down, turns, and sees you. His face lights up with a smile of such genuine, uncomplicated joy that it's almost painful to look at. "Visitors!" he calls. "Oh, welcome, welcome. You've come on a beautiful day."

## Setup
It is just after noon. [[Father Aldric]] is working his way down the high street, relighting every lantern snuffed overnight. Every door is barred, every window painted black. He is the only living soul in sight, and he is delighted to see the party.

This scene should feel wrong in small ways that add up: Aldric casts no shadow (DC 12 Perception, or automatically if a player asks about shadows); his skin has a pearly sheen; the air around him is warmer than the day; his lanterns hang in full sunlight; and the cat hates him. Let the players feel uneasy without being able to say why.

## NPC Goals
Aldric wants to welcome the newcomers, invite them to noon service ("My flock is resting just now, but they'll gather soon"), and, if [[Captain]] is present, get a good look at the "radiant creature." He doesn't want to hurt anyone. He believes everything he says.

**What Aldric says if asked:**
- **Where is everyone?** "Resting. It's been a hard summer for some. The light is a great comfort, but they don't always see it that way yet."
- **The barred doors?** A gentle sigh. "Fear. I knock every afternoon to bring the light to the sick. They'll open in time."
- **Wendel or the grain wagon?** "A carter? I don't recall. Perhaps Cooper's Row; the traffic goes that way." (True; he doesn't remember Wendel because he doesn't really see people any more.)
- **The graves?** "We've lost many this summer. But they're in the light now." He smiles, and means it.
- **Why light lanterns at noon?** He looks at them, puzzled, as if no one has ever asked. "Because it keeps us safe." A pause. "Doesn't it?"
- **Captain:** He stares a moment too long. *"Oh. Oh, what a radiant creature. You've brought a radiant creature."* He reaches toward the cat; Captain flattens and hisses. He withdraws his hand, hurt and fascinated.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Polite conversation:** Aldric invites them to noon service at the temple ("Just up the hill. The bell will ring.") and drifts on down the street, lighting lanterns. Captain refuses to follow. The party is free to explore; most will head to [[Cooper's Row]] to find the wagon.
- **The party attends noon service:** Captain will not go. The temple doors are open; inside, blinding light pours through a dome of stained glass. Aldric preaches warmly to empty pews, pausing as if listening to responses. DC 15 Perception: heat haze ripples above the pews in rows. *No one is attacked*: glarewights at service are docile. Let it be deeply unsettling, then let the party leave. (Don't describe the sanctum in detail; it's saved for [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]].)
- **The party attacks Aldric:** He doesn't fight back. Glarewights from the square defend him. Run [[Cooper's Row Ambush]] immediately using this street. Aldric walks away, untouched, and the hits leave pale, glassy marks he doesn't notice.
