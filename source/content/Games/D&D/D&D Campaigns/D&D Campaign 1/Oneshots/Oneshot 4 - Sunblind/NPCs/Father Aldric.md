**Role:** Villain (tragic)
**First appears:** Act 1
**Location:** [[Gildenfield Road]]

## Personality
- Unfailingly warm and pastoral; he speaks to everyone as a beloved member of his flock, including strangers and glarewights
- Serenely certain that light is goodness and that he is keeping Gildenfield safe; any contradiction slides off him like rain off glass
- At twilight only: frightened, guilt-stricken and painfully lucid, desperate for someone to stop what he can't

## Appearance
A tall, gentle man in faded saffron robes whose skin has the faint pearly sheen of a candle seen through paper, and who casts no shadow at all.

## Motivation
On the surface, Aldric wants to bring "the light" to every soul in Gildenfield: he lights lanterns, holds noon service for his "flock" (the glarewights), and knocks on bolted doors asking to "bring the light to the sick." He genuinely believes this is mercy.

Underneath, the deva-light he swallowed when the reliquary cracked is slowly replacing him. Most of what is left of Aldric is the routine he kept for thirty years: ring the dawn bell, hold noon service, tend the lamps, visit the sick. The light uses that routine to spread.

**His daily routine (use it to place him in any scene):**
- **Dawn:** rings the temple bell; lucid for a few minutes.
- **Noon:** holds service for a congregation of glarewights drifting into the pews.
- **Afternoon:** walks the streets relighting every lantern snuffed overnight, then knocks gently on bolted doors.
- **Dusk:** lucid for a few minutes, then retreats into the temple.
- **Night:** kneels motionless before the reliquary in the sanctum, humming. He doesn't sleep.

**Roleplaying him at noon (Act 1):** Warm, unhurried, a little too pleased to see the party. "Welcome, welcome! You've come on a beautiful day. My flock is resting just now, but you must come to noon service." He compliments sunburn as "the light's kiss." If [[Captain]] is present, he stares at the cat a moment too long, then says softly, *"Oh. Oh, what a radiant creature. You've brought a radiant creature."*

**Roleplaying him at dusk (Act 3, end):** The warmth drains out of his face and he looks his age. He speaks quickly, as if he has very little time: *"I kept it. It wanted to go, and I kept it. It sings in me now. I can't — at noon I can't — please. Please stop me."* Then the light comes back into his eyes and he smiles and wishes them a good evening.

## Secret
Aldric trapped a sliver of the deva **Ilvaris** in a mirrored reliquary at noon on a summer solstice, because he couldn't bear for the miracle that saved Gildenfield to leave. When the reliquary's crystal cracked during a midsummer storm, the escaping light became the first glarewights, and Aldric caught the rest of it in his own body trying to contain it. He is holding most of it back, and he doesn't know it. The lanterns he lights don't protect the town; they feed what is inside him.

His full stat block appears in [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]]. In this adventure he is not fought: if attacked, he doesn't retaliate, nearby glarewights defend him, and he walks away untouched. Hits leave pale, glassy marks on him that he doesn't notice.
