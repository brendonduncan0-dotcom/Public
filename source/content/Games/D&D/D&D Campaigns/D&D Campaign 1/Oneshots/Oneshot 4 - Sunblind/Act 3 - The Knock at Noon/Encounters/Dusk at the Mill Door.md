**Act:** 3 — [[Act 3 - The Knock at Noon]]
**Location:** [[Dunmore's Mill]]
**Encounter type:** Social

> [!info] Read Aloud
> As the sun slides behind the hills, there's a knock at the loading door. Not three soft taps this time: one, hesitant, like someone who isn't sure they're allowed. Through the crack you can see him. Father Aldric stands in the red evening light, and for the first time he looks like an old man. His face is grey and frightened, and he's twisting his hands together. "I'm sorry," he says, very fast, as if he doesn't have long. "I'm so sorry. Please listen."

## Setup
For a few minutes at dusk, Aldric is himself. He knows what he's done and what the light is doing to him. This is the first taste of his twilight lucidity; the full conversation happens in [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]]. Play it short, urgent and heartbreaking, then let the town arrive and turn the scene toward resolution and hope.

## NPC Goals
Aldric wants to warn them and beg them to stop him, before the light comes back.

**What he says (he has about one minute):**
- "I kept it. Ilvaris wanted to go, and I *kept* it. I thought I was saving us."
- "It's in me now. Most of it. I'm holding it, I think. I don't know for how long."
- "At noon I can't stop myself. At noon I don't *want* to."
- To Captain, or to Rook: he kneels, trembling. "Forgive me. Please. Tell — tell whoever you are — I'm sorry."
- Finally: *"Please. Stop me."*

Then the light comes back into his eyes. He straightens, smiles warmly, wishes them a good evening and a peaceful night, and walks back up the hill toward the temple.

## Possible Outcomes

**The town arrives.** As Aldric leaves, the town comes out at dusk and finds the mill standing and the party alive. Ned Dunmore and his family come up from the cellar. Mara, Dorrie, Osric (and the Fennimores, if Tam was treated) gather at the mill.

**The grain.** Ned and the townsfolk help load the grain. And then, rattling up the road, a brand-new wagon pulled by a very nervous mule: **[[Hobb Barleycorn]]**, white as a sheet, clutching a grain sack. "I found my nerve," he announces. "It was in a barrel. Long story. Is Wendel…?" Give him the news gently, whatever it is. If Wendel was buried properly, Hobb is grief-stricken but grateful, and pays 25 gp extra "for Wendel's sister, for the stone." **Levity:** if anyone mentions the barrel, Hobb insists it was "a strategic barrel."

**The contract is complete.** Hobb pays:
- **350 gp** for the grain (minus 8 gp per spoiled sack).
- **+100 gp bonus** if the party can give a full account (the journal or Aldric's confession).

**The next contract.** Mara, Dorrie and Osric step forward together with a heavy, clinking sack and a folded letter. They've pooled the town's savings. "This is for the guild. We want to hire you. Properly. To finish it." The letter is a formal contract request to the Faerûn Adventure League. **This leads into [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]].**

**Mara's ring.** If the party recovered Corwin's tin ring in [[Siege of Dunmore's Mill]], this is the moment to give it to her.

**Rest.** The party can finally take a long rest (in The Late Supper's cellar), which restores all lost Constitution.
