The night ends with a race to bury Wendel before dawn, in total darkness, by Osric's rite. Then the party holds Dunmore's Mill and Hobb's grain through the most dangerous hour of the day: noon. A gentle voice knocks at the door, and then the glarewights start bleaching the shutters away. This is where everything the party learned pays off: soot-blades, flour, shutters, darkness. At dusk, Aldric comes back himself for one terrible minute to beg them to stop him; the grain goes home, and the town hires the guild to finish the job.

## Scene 1 — Before Dawn

> [!tip] DM Cue
> A tense skill challenge in total darkness against the sunrise. If it fails, Wendel rises as a glarewight in the dim dawn light. Either way, Osric lends the black shovel to a party that stood beside him.

> [!tip] DM Cue
