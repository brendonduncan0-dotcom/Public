At first light, the party walks out to the field where the deva Ilvaris once stood, and finds Father Aldric kneeling on the wing-shaped imprint, himself for just a few minutes. This act is one conversation against the clock of the rising sun: what the party says here decides whether Aldric can be saved at noon. The twist lands here: the lanterns were never protecting the town; they were feeding the light, and Aldric has been holding most of it back all along. If Tam was never treated, he walks out of the wheat toward them as the sun rises, already turning. After the sun is up, the party has the morning to rest in the Long Cellar before the noon assault.

## Scene 1 — The Twilight Confession

> [!tip] DM Cue
> Six exchanges before sunrise. Count them. Let the players drive the conversation, and track Connections quietly. This scene is the emotional centre of both one-shots; don't rush it.
