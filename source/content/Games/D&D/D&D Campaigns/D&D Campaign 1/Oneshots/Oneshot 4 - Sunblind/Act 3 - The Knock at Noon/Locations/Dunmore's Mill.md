> [!info] Read Aloud
> The mill's ground floor is a big, dim, dusty room of heavy timber, every surface furred white with flour. A great millstone sits at its heart under a wooden hopper and grain chute. Through a wide doorway to the north, sacks of grain are stacked to the rafters. Thin blades of sunlight slide through the cracks around six shuttered windows, and through the floor you can hear the slow, steady creak of the waterwheel turning in the stream outside.

## Notable Features

- **The millstone:** a huge circular grinding stone under a wooden hopper in the centre of the main floor, waist high and solid.
- **The grain chute:** a slanted wooden chute from the hopper above, spilling a little flour with every turn of the wheel.
- **Six shutters:** two on the east wall, two on the west wall, one on the south wall beside the loading door, one on the north wall of the store room. All closed and barred.
- **The loading door:** a wide double door on the south wall, barred with a heavy beam.
- **Hobb's grain:** forty sacks stacked in the north store room, tagged *BARLEYCORN — PAID*.
- **Flour everywhere:** loose drifts in the corners, open sacks of milled flour along the walls; a cloud rises at every step.
- **The cellar hatch:** in the floor near the west wall, where Ned's family hides.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 10 Intelligence (or alchemist's supplies):** flour dust in the air is explosive near an open flame. (Make sure the party knows this; see [[Siege of Dunmore's Mill]].)
- **DC 12 Investigation (the cellar):** a narrow drainage tunnel leads from the cellar to the millstream, an escape route if the party must abandon the mill.
- **DC 13 Perception (shutters):** two of the shutters (one on the east wall, one on the west) are older and thinner than the others; they'll bleach faster (no bracing benefit).
- **DC 12 Investigation (store room):** spare tarps and planks behind the grain stacks, enough to cover three broken windows.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The mill is a fortress built of darkness. Keep a clear picture of which shutters are closed, bleaching or broken (tokens or a dry-erase mark on the battle mat work well). The light shafts from broken windows are the battlefield; the dark between them is the party's safe ground. Ned Dunmore and his wife and teenage daughter stay in the cellar during the fight and are never targeted.
