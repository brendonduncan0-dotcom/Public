**Role:** Ally (rumours and safe haven)
**First appears:** Act 2
**Location:** [[The Late Supper]]

## Personality
- Relentless gallows humour; she jokes about the sun the way sailors joke about drowning
- Motherly to everyone in a bullying sort of way: you will eat, you will sit, you will stop dripping on her floor
- Sharper than she lets on; she hears everything and remembers who said it

## Appearance
A broad, red-armed woman with her sleeves rolled to the shoulder and a chalkboard behind the bar that reads "SUNRISE IN: 6 HOURS. DRINK ACCORDINGLY."

## Motivation
Dorrie runs The Late Supper, the dusk-to-dawn tavern, which used to be The Early Riser until midsummer. She painted over the sign herself. She wants to keep her town fed, sane and laughing for as long as it takes, and she would very much like someone competent to finally *do* something.

**Lines and jokes to use:**
- "Welcome to The Late Supper. Breakfast is served at sunset, last orders at sunrise, and the vegetables are the best you'll ever eat, which is the most frightening thing in this town."
- "The priest? Lovely man. Christened half the town. Still knocks on my door every afternoon. I've stopped answering. Rude of me, I know."
- When someone asks about the weather: "Sunny. Always sunny. Gods, I miss rain."

**Rumours and clues she offers (one per round of drinks or kind word):**
- The trouble started at midsummer, the night of the big hailstorm. "Something cracked up at the temple. Heard it over the thunder, like a bell breaking."
- "Years back, in the bad winter, the dead walked out of the old barrow. The Father prayed and a tall stranger in white came and stopped it. Best summer we ever had after. Went on and on."
- "Don't bury anyone in the day. Ask Osric why. Actually, don't, he'll tell you."
- "Hollis Wick's still lighting lamps for the Father. Thinks more light will help. Man's a candle short of a chandelier."
- "The Fennimore boy got touched. Garrick won't let anyone in. You'll hear him through the door if you walk past."
- "If a merchant's cart came in two days back, it'll be on Cooper's Row. Nobody's touched it. Nobody goes on Cooper's Row."

## Secret
Dorrie's niece [[Pip]] saw "the bright man" up close and won't stop talking about it, and Dorrie is scared that saying so out loud will bring him knocking. She'll introduce the party to Pip only if they've been kind and patient.

## Pip's Aunt
Pip lives with Dorrie and helps behind the bar at night. Dorrie is fiercely protective of her; anyone who frightens Pip is out, and the town hears about it.
