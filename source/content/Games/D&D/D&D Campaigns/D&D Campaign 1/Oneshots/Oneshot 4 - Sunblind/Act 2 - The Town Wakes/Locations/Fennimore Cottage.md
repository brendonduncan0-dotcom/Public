> [!info] Read Aloud
> A neat farm cottage with a watch tabard hung over the porch rail and every shutter nailed shut. Inside, it's dark but for a soft, pearly glow from the back room, enough to see a spear propped by the door, a bucket of water nearly empty, and a young man lying in bed with a wet cloth over his eyes, his skin shining faintly like moonlight through milk.

## Notable Features

- **Nailed shutters:** every window boarded from the inside with fresh nails.
- **The barred door:** a heavy plank across iron brackets, plus a chair wedged under the handle.
- **Garrick's spear:** propped by the door, in reach of the chair where Garrick sleeps sitting up.
- **Tam's glow:** a soft, steady pearly light from his peeling skin, enough to read by.
- **The water bucket:** almost empty; a ladle beside it. Tam is always thirsty.
- **A soft cloth tie:** a strip of linen knotting Tam's wrist loosely to the bedframe.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Medicine (examining Tam):** no fever, no sweat at all, skin peeling in thin translucent sheets, eyes milky in bright light. He's not sick; he's *filling with light*.
- **DC 13 Perception (the front door):** scratch marks on the *inside* of the door at hand height, fresh. Someone has been trying to get *out*.
- **DC 12 Investigation (the mantel):** a small wooden toy horse with a note in a child's hand: "THANK YOU TAM." (From the neighbour's child Tam saved.)

> [!tip] DM Note
> This is the quietest, saddest scene of the night; play it gently. The cloth tie and the scratches on the door are the horror here, not anything gory: Tam is being called, and his father is holding on. Captain curls up on Tam's chest if allowed. If the party treats Tam, describe the glow dimming and his breathing slowing into real sleep. That's a victory worth as much as any fight.
