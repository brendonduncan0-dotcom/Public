**Role:** Neutral (red herring and source of friction)
**First appears:** Act 2
**Location:** [[Temple Vestry]]

## Personality
- Devoted to Father Aldric with the intensity of a man who has nothing else to believe in
- Nervy and earnest; talks with his hands, which are covered in small burn scars and mirror-cuts
- Stubbornly, tragically logical: "They hide in the light, so we need *more* light to see them"

## Appearance
A gangly man in his thirties with a pole-lantern over one shoulder and a belt hung with mirrors, tinder, wicks and a dozen keys that jangle as he walks.

## Motivation
Hollis is Gildenfield's lantern-keeper and Aldric's devoted follower. Years ago he helped the Father build "a reliquary for the miracle" (grinding lenses, silvering mirrors, cutting the frame) without fully understanding what it was for. Since midsummer he has been rigging mirror-lanterns all over town, convinced that flooding an area with light will expose the "sun-ghosts" so people can fight them. He wants to protect the town and to prove that the Father is still the Father.

He is wrong in the most dangerous possible way: his rigs hide the glarewights instead of revealing them. His mirror-and-lantern tripwires all over town are his work, not Mara's, and the two of them are not on speaking terms.

**What he can tell the party (if they're patient and don't insult Aldric):**
- He helped build the reliquary: "Mirrors and lenses, a cage of light. Noon of the solstice, all the sun in the world through one crystal. It was *beautiful*."
- The Father has been "unwell" since the storm, "but that's the light working through him."
- The Father doesn't sleep at night. He prays before the reliquary in the sanctum until dawn.

## Secret
Hollis saw what happened at the solstice: the tall stranger in white screaming as its light was torn away. He has told himself ever since that he imagined it. Any PC who presses him with evidence (the journal, a successful DC 15 Insight followed by a gentle question) can make him break down and admit it.

**In [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]]**, Hollis floods Lantern Square with light during the night market, believing it will help. Whether the party has been kind to him here affects whether he can be talked out of it.
