At dusk, Gildenfield wakes. Doors open, lanterns are held up to strangers' faces, and the party is welcomed, warily, into a town that has learned to live by night. This act is the investigation: earning the town's trust, getting the tools to fight back (Mara's soot-blades and sunburn remedy), meeting the glowing son, and stealing Aldric's journal from under his nose. By the end of the night the party should understand what Aldric did, know how to bury Wendel properly, and know that they'll need to hold the mill at noon tomorrow. The leads after Scene 2 can be taken in any order; keep one eye on the sunrise chalkboard.
