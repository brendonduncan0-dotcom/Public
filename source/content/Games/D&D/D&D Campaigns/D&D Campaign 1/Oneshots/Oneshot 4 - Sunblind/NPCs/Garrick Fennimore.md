**Role:** Neutral (moral test)
**First appears:** Act 2
**Location:** [[Fennimore Cottage]]

## Personality
- Grim and exhausted; hasn't slept properly in weeks
- Fiercely protective of his son; will put himself between Tam and anything, including the party
- Proud and ashamed in equal measure: he was the town watch captain and he couldn't protect his own family

## Appearance
A broad, grey-stubbled man in a battered watch tabard, who keeps a spear propped by the door and a wet cloth over his son's eyes.

## Motivation
Garrick's son [[Tam Fennimore]] went out at noon a fortnight ago to drag a neighbour's child into shelter, and was touched by glarewights three times doing it. Tam saved the child; the sun sickness is slowly killing him. Garrick has barred the door to everyone since, terrified that the town will decide Tam is "turning" and take him away, or that the priest will come knocking and Tam will open the door.

He wants his son to live, and he won't believe anyone who says that's possible until they prove it.

## Secret
Garrick has heard Tam whispering "let him in" in his sleep, in the afternoons, when the knocking comes. He hasn't told anyone, and he's tied Tam's wrist to the bedframe with a soft cloth so he can't reach the door.
