**Role:** Ally (gruff)
**First appears:** Act 2
**Location:** [[The Late Supper]] (at night); [[Gildenfield Graveyard]] (before dawn)

## Personality
- Gruff, monosyllabic and bone-tired; he answers questions with a shrug and a grunt until he decides you're worth his breath
- Takes the dead very seriously and the living much less so
- A dry, deadpan wit that surprises people ("Business is good. Wish it wasn't.")

## Appearance
A stooped, white-bearded man with earth permanently ground into the creases of his hands, who carries an old black-bladed shovel everywhere, even to the tavern.

## Motivation
Osric has been Gildenfield's gravedigger for forty years and dug the graves in the bad winter, when the dead walked out of the old barrow. He works nights by choice now. He wants every one of Gildenfield's dead to *stay* dead, and he is losing.

**The burial rite (he'll explain it to anyone who asks properly):** a body killed by the sun-ghosts must be buried **before dawn**, **in total darkness**, with its **eyes wrapped in soot-blackened cloth** and the **body packed in cellar mud**. Done right, it stays down. Done late, or in daylight, it rises at the next dawn as one of them.

## Secret
Osric keeps a **burial ledger**: every burial since midsummer, with time, method and whether it held. The ledger shows twenty-three burials, seven failed. One failed entry reads, in his cramped hand: *"Corwin Thatch. Buried 2nd hr past noon by his widow's wish. Rose."* He has never shown it to Mara, because he knows she knows.

## The Black Shovel
Osric's shovel was used to dig the graves in the bad winter, and the barrow's grave-chill never left its blade. If the party helps him with a proper burial (see [[Before Dawn]]), he lends it to them "until this is done":

- **Black Shovel:** simple melee weapon, 1d6 bludgeoning plus 1d4 necrotic damage, counts as magical against glarewights. The grave-chill fades once the glarewights are gone; it's a loan, not loot.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The shovel is a great tool for any PC whose damage is mostly radiant or nonmagical. Steer it toward whoever has had the worst time in the [[Cooper's Row Ambush]].
