**Role:** Witness
**First appears:** Act 2
**Location:** [[The Late Supper]]

## Personality
- Matter-of-fact in the eerie, simple way of children who say true things adults won't
- Intensely curious about the party, especially anything with fur or a sword
- Brave about the wrong things and scared of the wrong things: unbothered by glarewights, terrified of soup with lumps

## Appearance
An eight-year-old girl with a gap-toothed grin, a charcoal stick behind one ear, and chalk dust on everything she owns.

## Motivation
Pip wants someone to *believe her*. A month ago she saw "the bright man" up close through a crack in the cellar shutter. Every adult has told her to hush. She'll talk to anyone who listens, and especially to anyone who brings [[Captain]] over to say hello.

**What Pip says (in her words):**
- "The Father used to have a shadow. Now he doesn't. I checked."
- "He was inside the light. Like a candle inside a lantern. Except backwards."
- "The bright things go to church. At dinnertime. They sit in the seats."
- "He knocked on our door and said 'Is anyone sick, little one?' He said it the same every time. Like a bird."
- She shows them a charcoal drawing: the temple, with a big shining shape inside it, and the Father standing *in front* of the shape with his arms spread, as if he's holding a door shut. (This is the twist, foreshadowed by a child. Don't explain it.)

## Secret
Pip doesn't think the bright man is bad. "He looked sad. Like when you can't find your mum." This is a seed for Aldric's redemption.

> [!tip] DM Note
> Pip is never in danger in this adventure. She is a witness only. Keep her safely in The Late Supper and its cellar.
