**Role:** Neutral (the deva; appears only if freed)
**First appears:** Act 3
**Location:** [[The Sun Temple]]

## Personality
- Gentle, unhurried and warm, with a voice "like warm bread," exactly as Aldric's journal described
- Utterly without anger; Ilvaris has had a long time to be afraid, and has chosen not to be
- Quietly, profoundly tired

## Appearance
A tall, thin figure of soft gold light in the shape of a robed man with folded wings, translucent and diminished, like a candle seen through morning mist.

## Motivation
Ilvaris is a deva who answered Aldric's prayer in the bad winter, drove back the dead, and stayed a season out of kindness. When it tried to leave, Aldric tore a sliver of its light away at the solstice. The rest of Ilvaris fled "grey and small" and has been diminished ever since. Ilvaris wants to be whole and to go home. It does not want revenge.

If the party frees the light (see [[The Choice]]), Ilvaris reforms under the open sky, diminished but whole, and:
- **Thanks the party** with a blessing: every PC gains Heroic Inspiration and is healed of all sun sickness; every touched person in Gildenfield, including [[Tam Fennimore]], is healed.
- **Forgives Aldric**, if he is alive. "You held it shut as long as you could. That is not nothing." If Aldric is dying, Ilvaris touches his forehead and he wakes, burned and human, weeping.
- **Recognises Captain.** A long, silent look between them. Ilvaris bows to the cat. If Rook asks, Ilvaris says only: "Your friend lent me some of their light, long ago. I'm sorry I lost it." (Or reframe this to fit whatever relationship you've chosen between Captain and Ilvaris.)
- **Leaves.** "Light is not meant to be kept." It rises through the broken dome and is gone.

## Secret
Ilvaris knows who the "kind stranger at the market in Waterdeep" was, the one who taught Aldric the mirror geometry. It says only: "He wanted to see if it could be done. He will want to see if it can be done again." A sequel hook; leave it hanging.
