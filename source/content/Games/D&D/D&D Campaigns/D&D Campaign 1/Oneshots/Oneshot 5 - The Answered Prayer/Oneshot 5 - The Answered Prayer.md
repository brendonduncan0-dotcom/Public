## Premise

Years ago, Father Aldric of Gildenfield prayed for help and a deva answered, and when it tried to leave, he caged a sliver of its light in a mirrored reliquary. That light has cracked loose: it hunts the town by day as glarewights and burns inside Aldric himself, who is lucid only at twilight and begs to be stopped. Gildenfield has pooled its savings to hire the guild to end it. The party has one night to prepare, one dawn to reach the man inside the light, and one noon to walk into the blazing sun temple and decide what becomes of the deva's stolen light.

## Themes

- **Redemption and its limits:** can a man who did something terrible out of love be saved, and should he be?
- **Light as danger, darkness as safety:** the final inversion, where the party must make a holy place dark to save it
- **Letting go:** "light is not meant to be kept"; grief, possessiveness, and the courage to release what you love
- **Community:** a frightened town pooling everything it has, and finally stepping into the sun
- **Mild body horror:** sunburn, glowing skin, a young man turning into light; never graphic
- **A patron's private stake:** the warlock's patron has a personal connection to the trapped light, and a quiet agenda of its own

## Hooks

- **Primary — Guild contract:** The townsfolk of Gildenfield (led by Mara Thatch, Dorrie Nightjar and Osric Loam) have pooled their savings, and the Waterdeep temple of Lathander has added a bounty after reading the party's report. The guild posts the contract: end the glarewight threat in Gildenfield. **Reward: 500 gp. Bonus: +100 gp if Father Aldric is brought back alive.**
- **Alternative — Fresh contract:** If this group didn't play [[Oneshot 4 - Sunblind]], the guild's contracts officer briefs them using the summary in [[The Town's Contract]]: a town that hides by day, a priest gone strange, and a previous guild team's report of invisible radiant creatures.
- **Alternative — The patron's call:** Rook dreams three nights running of a warm voice calling a name he doesn't know, *Ilvaris*, and wakes with Captain sitting on his chest, staring toward the east. Captain won't eat until they go.

## Possible Endings

- **Full Victory (Freed):** The relic entity is destroyed, Aldric survives (reached at twilight and talked down), and the party frees Ilvaris under the open sky. The deva reforms, diminished but whole, forgives Aldric, and heals all sun sickness in Gildenfield. The town steps into daylight for the first time in months. Full reward plus the Aldric bonus.
- **Victory (Patron or Shard):** The entity is destroyed, but the party gives the light to Captain (Rook's patron speaks for the first time; sweet or ominous at the DM's discretion) or keeps the shard (it is flagged for the guild auction, still holding a captive light: a sequel hook). The glarewights fade either way. Aldric may or may not survive.
- **Tragedy:** Aldric is never reached and dies in the sanctum; the party wins but buries him before dawn by Osric's rite. The town is free, but quiet. Full reward, no bonus.
- **Failure / Retreat:** The party is driven out of the sun temple. Retreat is always possible: the glarewights won't follow into darkness, and the entity can't leave the temple while its light is anchored to the reliquary. The town remains in hiding, and the guild can send them back after a rest.

## DM Notes

**Emotional shape:** resolve → tenderness → catharsis. Act 1 is a "gearing up" act with warmth and community (the town gathering everything it has for the party), broken by a chaotic night battle. Act 2 is the quiet heart of the two-parter: one conversation at dawn that decides whether Aldric lives. Act 3 is the payoff of every lesson from both sessions: make a holy place dark, then face what the light really is.

**The twist to land:** *the lanterns never protected the town; they fed the thing. Aldric was holding it back all along.* Seed it in Act 2's confession ("I think I'm holding it. I don't know how long."), confirm it in Act 3 when Aldric falls and the light pours out of him into something much bigger and much worse.

**The Light Level track** is the core mechanic of the finale (see [[The Sun Temple]] and [[The Noon Service]]). Keep it visible to the players: a d6 on the table showing the current number of lit sources works perfectly. Every point the party knocks off should be cheered.

**Preparation pays off.** Track every resource the party gathers in [[Preparing for Noon]] (smoke pots, flour bombs, soot cloths, the Darkness scroll). Each has a specific use in the finale. If the party prepared well, the finale should feel like a heist going to plan.

**Radiant damage** still heals glarewights and the relic entity, and still bleaches rather than burns (see [[Oneshot 4 - Sunblind]]'s DM Notes). Rook should be using Eldritch Blast and anything necrotic; Osric's [[Osric Loam#The Black Shovel|Black Shovel]] is the best weapon in the party.

**Captain** refused to go near the temple in Oneshot 4. In Act 3, Captain walks in on its own. Make that a moment. In Phase 2, the entity tries to draw Captain's light into itself; protecting the cat becomes an objective. In the ending, Captain may speak for the first and only time.

**Recurring NPCs** from Oneshot 4 ([[Mara Thatch]], [[Dorrie Nightjar]], [[Osric Loam]], [[Hollis Wick]], [[Tam Fennimore]], [[Garrick Fennimore]], [[Pip]], [[Captain]], [[Glarewight]]) are linked from that one-shot's notes rather than duplicated here. **Levity beats:** Hobb Barleycorn insisting on donating "moral support" (a sack of flour, labelled); Dorrie updating her chalkboard to "NOON IN: 14 HOURS. DRINK ACCORDINGLY"; the town's earnest "darkness drill"; Cordelia teaching a nocturnal town to curtsey; the cat walking into the temple like it owns the place.

**Common derailments:**
- **The party skips the twilight conversation:** Aldric can't be talked down in Phase 1 without being reached (DC 20 instead of DC 12), and the entity starts Phase 2 at full HP. Still winnable.
- **The party attacks Aldric at dawn:** He doesn't fight back; he lets them. If they kill him at dawn, the light streams back up the hill into the reliquary, and at noon the party faces the Relic at full strength with no Phase 1 and no chance to save him (see [[The Twilight Confession]]). Telegraph it first ("If I fall, it all comes out").
- **The party tries to destroy the reliquary at night:** The reliquary is in the sanctum with Aldric kneeling before it and the glarewights roosting. Aldric isn't lucid at night and simply blocks them, smiling. Glarewights attack if anyone touches the reliquary. It's best done at noon, when the party can control the light.
