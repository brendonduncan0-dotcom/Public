> [!info] Read Aloud
> A narrow lane of workshops runs north between leaning two-storey buildings, its cobbles shimmering with heat. Faded canvas awnings sag over the shopfronts on the east side, throwing the only shade in sight. Across the lane the double doors of a cooperage hang open on a darkness so total it looks painted on. In the middle of the street stands an abandoned grain wagon, and behind it, a bleached-white wall with a man-shaped patch of old red brick.

## Notable Features

- **Wendel's wagon:** a two-axle grain wagon with empty traces, six sacks of flour still in the bed, and a carter's ledger on the seat.
- **The silhouette wall:** the bakery's west-facing wall, bleached white and faintly glassy, except for a red-brick man-shape with arms thrown up.
- **East-side awnings:** sun-faded canvas awnings over a bakery, a chandler's and a saddler's; the only shade on the street.
- **The cooperage:** wide double doors open onto a dark workshop full of barrel staves, hoops and a cold forge. Inside is total darkness.
- **Ball bearings:** glinting steel balls scattered across the cooperage and bakery thresholds.
- **Bell strings and tin cans:** strung across three front doors on the east side.
- **A lantern post at the north end:** a tall post hung with four lanterns and angled hand-mirrors, aimed south down the street.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 13 Perception:** a fine tripwire runs across the north end of the street to the lantern post (the mirror-lantern rig). DC 12 thieves' tools disarms it.
- **DC 13 Perception:** straw scattered beside the wagon's south wheel hides a set bear trap.
- **DC 12 Medicine or Investigation (Wendel's body under the wagon):** no wounds, no burns. The skin is peeled and pale, the eyes milky, as if he had been left in the sun for a hundred years in one afternoon. He has been dead less than a day.
- **DC 10 Investigation (wagon seat):** Wendel's ledger, last entry: *"Mill shut. Town shut. Priest says wait for service. Will wait in cooperage shade till dusk."*
- **DC 14 Perception (in the cooperage):** soot-blackened handprints on the inside of the door, and a pot of lamp-black. Someone has been coating staves here; a clue toward soot-blades.
- **Detect Magic:** nothing; the glarewights don't register.

> [!tip] DM Note
> Cooper's Row is designed as a classroom for the light rules: bright street, dim awning shade, dark cooperage, all within a few squares of each other. Keep the three zones vivid in your descriptions. The flour sacks in the wagon are the party's first "reveal grenades"; if no one thinks of them, have a wight brush a sack and come out dusted white. Wendel's body needs to go somewhere dark and safe until it can be buried; the cooperage is ideal.
