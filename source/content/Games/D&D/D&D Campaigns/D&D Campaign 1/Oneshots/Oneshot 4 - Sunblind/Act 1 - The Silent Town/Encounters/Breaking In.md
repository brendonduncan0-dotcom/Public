**Act:** 1 — [[Act 1 - The Silent Town]]
**Location:** [[The Chandler's Cellar]]
**Encounter type:** Combat (optional, ends in social)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The cellar door gives way with a crack, and the stairs below fall away into perfect darkness. Then there is a sound like a hundred tiny marbles rolling, a bell jangling wildly somewhere ahead, and a thin, cracked voice shrieking, "It's in! *It's in!* Hit it, Bertram, HIT IT!"

## Setup
**Optional.** Run this only if the party forces a door by day, usually seeking shelter after [[Cooper's Row Ambush]] or trying to find someone to talk to. The chandler's family (old Agnes Rush, her son Bertram, and his wife Hettie) are hiding in their cellar. They're convinced anything coming through a door in daylight is a sun-ghost.

The cellar is in **total darkness**. The household's traps are already set:
- **Ball bearings** across the bottom three steps: DC 10 Dex save or fall Prone (and tumble to the bottom).
- **Bell string** across the stair: rings when crossed, and the family attacks.
- **Flour bucket** balanced over the inner door: the first creature through is coated white (harmless, and very funny).

## Objectives
- End the fight without killing anyone.
- Prove to the family that the party is human.

## Enemies
| Creature | CR | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Gildenfield Villager]] | 0 | 3 | Bertram has the hunting bow; Agnes has a rolling pin; Hettie has a candle-snuffer on a pole |

**Terrain features:** See [[The Chandler's Cellar]]. Darkness throughout unless the party brings light, and bringing light makes the family *more* convinced they're ghosts.

> [!warning] Scaling
> This is a frightened family, not a real threat. If the party hits hard, the villagers drop unconscious and the rest surrender immediately. If the party is clever (speaking calmly in the dark, showing a bleeding cut, pointing out their own shadows by a shuttered lantern), end the fight before initiative is rolled.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Proved human peacefully:** The family is mortified and grateful. Agnes shares a secret: "The sun-ghosts can't abide the dark. Mara Thatch says soot on a blade hurts them." No trust penalty, as long as the party repairs or barricades the broken door before leaving.
- **Fought, no one killed:** The family surrenders in tears. **Town trust −1** (see [[The Lantern Inspection]]): the story is all over Gildenfield by dusk.
- **Someone killed:** **Town trust −2** and Mara refuses to deal with the party at all until they make amends (bringing the body to [[Osric Loam]] for proper burial counts).
- **Levity beat:** Whoever is coated in flour will be "revealed" as the ghost by Agnes, who swings at them with the rolling pin and won't be convinced until they sneeze.
