**Act:** 2 — [[Act 2 - The Twilight Confession]]
**Location:** [[Ilvaris's Field]]
**Encounter type:** Social

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The sky is grey and the field is silver with dew. Out among the wheat, in a circle where nothing grows at all, a man is kneeling with his back to you. As you come closer you see the ground beneath him: bare earth baked pale and glassy in the shape of two great outspread wings. Father Aldric doesn't turn around. "You came," he says, and his voice is cracked and ordinary and terribly afraid. "Good. Sit, if you like. We haven't long. When the sun clears the hills, I'll be gone again."

## Setup
Every dawn, before he rings the bell, Aldric walks out to the field where Ilvaris first stood and kneels on the wing-shaped imprint. For the few minutes between first light and sunrise, he is entirely himself. This conversation is the emotional heart of the two-parter, and it decides whether Aldric can be saved.

**The clock:** the party has **six exchanges** (a question or statement and Aldric's answer) before the sun clears the hills. Count them out loud if you like; dawn light creeping across the field is a great visual timer. On the sixth, describe the light touching his face, and the warmth returning to his smile.

## Objectives
- Learn the truth about the reliquary and the light.
- **Reach Aldric:** earn **3 Connections** before sunrise.

## NPC Goals
Aldric wants to confess, to be stopped, and, though he won't say it, to be forgiven. He's terrified of what he'll do at noon.

## What Aldric Knows

Give him these as answers, in his own frightened, honest voice:
- **On the light:** "It's not evil. It's *hungry*. It was part of something whole, and I tore it away, and it wants to be bright and warm and big again. It wants noon forever."
- **The twist:** "The lanterns. I thought they kept us safe. They don't. They *feed* it. Every lamp I light makes it stronger, and I can't stop lighting them."
- **What he's doing:** "I think I'm holding it. Most of it. Inside me, like my hands over the crack. If I fall, it all comes out at once. Be ready for that."
- **The reliquary:** "The crystal's still on the altar, cracked. What's left of Ilvaris's light is anchored to it. Break it under the open sky and the light can go home. Or... the light can be *given*." (He glances at Captain.)
- **The sanctum at noon:** "Darkness. Make it dark. Every window, every lamp. I'll try to stop you. Don't let me."
- **Captain:** he looks at the cat for a long moment and bows his head. "You know what I did. I'm so sorry." Captain stares back, unblinking.

## Skill Challenge: Reaching Aldric

Each of these earns **1 Connection** (each only once). Three Connections before sunrise means Aldric is **reached**.

| Approach | Check | DC | Connection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speak the name **Ilvaris** with kindness, not accusation | — | — | Automatic. He flinches, then weeps. |
| Show him his own journal (from Oneshot 4) | — | — | Automatic. "I don't remember the last pages. That frightens me more than anything." |
| Tell him he can still choose: "At noon, let go." | Persuasion | 13 | He nods slowly. "If you make it dark enough, I think I could hear you." |
| Understand him: ask *why* | Insight | 12 | He tells them about the bad winter, the children he buried. "I couldn't go back to that. I couldn't." |
| Offer forgiveness, or say Ilvaris might forgive him | Persuasion or Religion | 14 | "Do you think — no. I don't deserve it. But thank you." |
| Captain approaches him and sits at his knee | — | — | Automatic, if Rook allows it. Aldric puts a trembling hand near the cat but doesn't touch it. |
| Promise to free Ilvaris, not keep the light | Persuasion | 12 | "Then I'll help you, if I can find myself at noon." |

## Possible Outcomes
- **Reached (3+ Connections):** At noon, Aldric's Twilight's Echo DC is **12** instead of 20 (see [[Aldric, the Radiant Revenant]]). If talked down, he tears the light out of himself on his own terms: **the Relic of Ilvaris starts Phase 2 with 30 fewer hit points**, and Aldric survives. As the sun rises, he whispers, "Make it dark. Say my name. I'll try to hear you."
- **Not reached:** Aldric still gives them everything he knows, but at noon he can be talked down only at DC 20. As the sun rises: "I'm sorry. I'm so sorry. Don't let me—" and then the warm, serene smile, and he walks to the temple to ring the bell.
- **The party attacks him:** He doesn't fight back: "Yes. Maybe that's better." Telegraph clearly: "If I fall, it all comes out." If they kill him anyway, the light tears out of his body in a blinding column (each creature within 15 feet: DC 14 Constitution save or Blinded until the end of its next turn) and streams up the hill into the reliquary. At noon, the party faces [[The Relic Unbound]] in the sanctum at full HP with no Phase 1, and the Light Level starts at 6 (the Relic relit everything). There is no Aldric bonus, and no chance to save him.
- **Tam arrives:** If Tam was never treated, he walks out of the wheat during the conversation; see [[The Son Turns]].
