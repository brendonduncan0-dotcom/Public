**Role:** Ally (once trusted)
**First appears:** Act 2
**Location:** [[Mara's House]]

## Personality
- Blunt and practical to the point of rudeness; she doesn't waste words, lamp oil or sympathy
- Proud of her traps and quietly ashamed of the ones that hurt the wrong people
- Half-right about everything: she has worked out most of the rules by trial and grief, and is wrong about why

## Appearance
A lean woman in her forties with soot-black streaks on her face like war paint and a bandolier of flour pouches across her chest.

## Motivation
Mara's husband, Corwin, was a trapper too. Three months ago he went out at noon to check snares at the field's edge and never came back; the town found only a bleached silhouette on a fence. Mara set most of Gildenfield's traps since then, by trial and error: flour and ash to reveal footprints, ball bearings to trip something that walks, bear traps for a "beast." She wants the thing that killed Corwin dead, and she wants her town to stop dying.

She has worked out that soot on a blade hurts the "sun-ghosts," and that a paste of cellar mud, cold ash and nightshade eases sunburn sickness. She thinks the glarewights are the ghosts of the dead "taken by the sun." That's half-right: they're the light, not the dead, though the dead do rise as more of them.

**What she gives a party that earns her trust:**
- **The soot-blade lesson:** how to coat weapons in soot or lamp-black pitch so they count as magical against glarewights for one fight. She gives the party a [[Mara Thatch#Mara's Soot Kit|soot kit]].
- **The sunburn remedy:** cellar mud, cold ash and nightshade paste, applied at night, restores `dice: 1d4` lost Constitution.
- **A warning:** "Don't fight them in the sun. You can't win in the sun. Drag them into the dark, or go inside and make them come to you."

## Secret
Mara knows one of the glarewights is Corwin. She buried him in daylight because she couldn't bear to wait, and he burst out of his grave the next dawn. She has never told anyone, and she checks [[Osric Loam]]'s burial ledger every week to see if anyone has noticed. If the party learns this gently and lays Corwin to rest (in [[Siege of Dunmore's Mill]], the glarewight wearing a tin wedding ring), Mara's gratitude is total.

## Mara's Soot Kit
A battered tin of lamp-black, a pot of pitch and a rag. **6 uses.** As an action, a creature can coat one weapon (or a bundle of up to 20 pieces of ammunition) with one use; that weapon's attacks count as magical against glarewights until the end of the next fight. Rindle can make 2 more uses with an hour's work and alchemist's supplies (DC 12 Intelligence check). The kit is a consumable, not a magic item.
