**Act:** 3 — [[Act 3 - The Knock at Noon]]
**Location:** [[Dunmore's Mill]]
**Encounter type:** Social (tension)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> It's close to noon. The mill is dark and close and smells of flour and old wood, and thin blades of sunlight press through the cracks in the shutters. Below your feet, the miller's family is silent in the cellar. Then, very gently, there is a knock on the loading door. Three soft taps. A pause. And a voice, warm and kind and patient, like a grandfather at a sickbed: "Hello? Is anyone sick in there? I've come to bring the light." Another pause. "And I would so like to see your radiant creature again."

## Setup
After the burial, the party shelters through the morning in [[Dunmore's Mill]] with the grain they're contracted to collect; Ned Dunmore and his family hide in the cellar beneath. The party can take a short rest here during the morning, but not a long one before noon.

At noon, [[Father Aldric]] comes knocking. He's drawn to [[Captain]], the "radiant creature," and his flock follows him. This scene is about tension, not combat: build dread for 5–10 minutes before the shutters start to go.

## Objectives
- Decide how to handle the knock: hide, talk, or fight.
- Prepare the mill before the glarewights come in.

## NPC Goals
Aldric wants to be let in. He wants to "bring the light to the sick" and to see the radiant creature. He's endlessly patient, endlessly kind, and absolutely will not leave.

## Skill Challenge

| Approach | Check | DC | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hide in silence** | Group Stealth | 13 | Aldric knocks again, sighs, and says "I'll wait." He hums. A minute later, the first shutter starts to bleach. The party gets a free round of preparation before initiative. |
| **Talk through the door** | Persuasion or Deception | 14 | Aldric is happy to chat. He describes the light as a gift, his flock as his children. The party can ask him anything; see below. |
| **Say "Ilvaris" through the door** | — | — | The humming stops. A long silence. Then, in a small, frightened, *lucid* voice: "...How do you know that name?" A heartbeat later: "No. No, the light is good. Let me in." It shows the party that the man is still in there. |
| **Captain** | — | — | Captain sits facing the door, absolutely still, tail wrapped around its paws. It is not afraid. It is *grieving*. |
| **Fight from inside** (attack through the door, open it) | — | — | Aldric doesn't fight. Opening the door floods the mill with sunlight and lets the glarewights in at once. Start the siege with the loading door open. |

**What Aldric says if engaged through the door:**
- "They're not monsters. They're light. They just want to be close to warm things. Is that so terrible?"
- "I've knocked on every door in this town. No one ever opens. I don't understand why."
- If asked about the journal: a long silence. "I don't... I don't remember writing that." (Insight DC 12: he's telling the truth, and it frightens him.)

## Possible Outcomes
- **In every case**, after a few minutes Aldric's voice says, gently, "Well. If you won't open the door, my flock will help." The shutters begin to bleach. Run [[Siege of Dunmore's Mill]].
- **Preparation:** however the scene goes, give the party a moment to prepare: stack grain sacks, blacken weapons, fill pockets with flour, decide who guards which shutter. A party that prepared well should feel it in the fight.
- **The party learns he's still in there:** if they used Ilvaris's name, the lucid moment sets up [[Dusk at the Mill Door]] and the twilight conversation in [[Oneshot 5 - The Answered Prayer]].
